## 4. Findings that change the next plan

- **Inventory, not universal editability:** 892 successful selected Record View captures exposed 118 named keys; Guest Details panel had 84 named keys plus four unnamed controls. Thirty selected stays had 33 guest forms with 78 controls each. One blank Guest Stay had 108 named controls. The audit crosswalk covers these captured surfaces separately. Hidden controls and unloaded pages remain unknown. `readOnly=false` is not proof of editability or persistence; 11 of the 65 controls carrying that flag were buttons.
- **Address and phone:** synthetic values were accepted locally on a guarded blank draft after fill and Tab. No mutating HTTP request was observed and no save was clicked. This does not prove persistence, shared-profile ownership or saved-record editing. In 30 identity-matched saved records, six phone values appeared in the guest form where Record View was blank; seven mobiles appeared only in Record View; one mobile and one email differed. Preserve source and conflict rather than guessing authority.
- **Agency, organization and A/R:** separate concepts. In the selected 892 records, agency was populated in 596, organization in 548 and A/R in 174. The current strict create accepts verified agency and/or organization proofs but requires A/R blank; it refuses a selected A/R rather than clearing it. Company-only historical Labtest create/readback exists; agency plus company is not established.
- **CMS and guarantee:** CMS Message Id `03` appeared in 840/892 selected records, including 51 without a populated agency or organization. It is useful evidence, not a universal default. A 2026-10-28 owner rule said CMS Message Id must never be blank on a Nava create; the historical HRDAV blank default conflicted with it. Current HRCEN default is unverified and current create has no explicit nonblank guard. `NGI` is a **guarantee type**, not a rate; the current Labtest writer rejects other guarantees. Do not hard-code one rate.
- **Children:** preserve explicit zero, known count with unknown ages, and known ages separately. In the selected 892 records, 868 had zero children, 16 one and eight two; the 13–17 age band appeared in three records. A source-level helper may collapse unknown ages to an empty list, but other paths reject null ages; reachability and live impact are unverified. Do not claim a production incident.
- **Saved writes and strict proof:** existing saved Cloud amendment is narrow: note and Booker fields have historical Labtest fresh-readback evidence. One paired Booker save read back despite an `uncertain` tool outcome. Older create documentation says a strict full-body path had not been dispatched; later run records document successful fake creates, including occupancy/source and a company-selected example. Their exact execution SHA was not recorded. The failed `revenuevalue` contents and cause were not retained. Do not claim a specific serialization fix or present old documentation as current proof. The 985-key matcher may be brittle, but unknown fields can carry side effects; do not weaken safety without classification.
- **Partial price and stock:** an older staff BAR projection averages only positive observed nightly prices and takes the minimum of non-null observed stock, without requested-night coverage. A separate sellability path requires complete data. Cloud availability views already retain nightly prices, counts and restrictions. Preserve useful partial data and label coverage; missing is not zero or sold out. Do not represent an observed subset average or minimum as a verified full-stay value.
- **Background quote work:** a queued quote job, persisted result and pane readback exist when enabled. Pane-side polling stops on cleanup. Cloud quote snapshot handles are page-scoped and deleted on page close; current Cloud-to-pane integration, cross-session recovery and effective runtime flags are unverified. The owner wants staff to navigate away and return without losing useful work.
- **Finance and documents:** existing Cloud Record View and shallow cards expose displayed amounts; finance preflight projects them, not ledger transactions or cleared funds. A typed folio reader exists, but live access and transaction completeness are not established. The Cloud documents reader returns loaded metadata, not bytes or upload. A voucher is often a booking/billing instruction, not proof funds cleared. A future new Cloud uploader may serve several workflows; the old uploader is reference only.
- **Multi-room:** 24 same-profile/date clusters containing 61 selected records with different assigned rooms are a useful Centurion pilot signal. They do not prove association or population prevalence. Historical HRDAV evidence showed two linked fake rooms with separate confirmations. The owner wants the earlier deferral reassessed. The 892 snapshot did not capture association grids, and a separate 60-stay grid sample had questionable completeness.
- **Parked scope:** OCR, proof-of-payment extraction and intake/triage wiring are parked. General HMS document and finance capabilities remain in consideration. Group/XLSX work is parked; a future group-PDF investigation has its own to-do. Do not silently widen scope.
