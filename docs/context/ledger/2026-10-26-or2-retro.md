## 4. Why 240 min was wrong

| What the basis assumed | What happened | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| 22 units | The plan at 01:40 had **30** T1 units; live P0 work added 4 more, for 34 | ETA never re-quoted after the plan; no new ledger estimate row |
| Longest chain of 6 units | The plan's own DAG said **13 slices → 455 min plus an unpriced P0 tail** (plan §DAG, line 670). The actual chain had 11 links | ~+175–250 min |
| ~35 min per link (code 10–20 + clean PR ~18) | **~50 min per link**: lane cold start + gates + ~5 min review + 90 s tick, and the next unit starts only after the merge | ~+165 min |
| Coding starts immediately | 85 min of prep inside the window | +85 min |
| P1 tail treated as a "risk" | 46 Greptile P1s on 19 of 41 PRs; Lease-R took 4 rounds; DS3-C went to round 3 | included in the per-link time |
| — | DV live proof ran serially in staff hours, at DEV priority behind prefill | bar still open |

Plainly: this is the **same mistake as mh1024 #5**. We quoted a number from a basis that did not match the run we then executed. The run's own plan had the right number (~7h44 + tail), and nobody used it. The speed claim itself held up:

| Metric | or2 | mh1024 (10-25) | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| PRs merged / wall | 39 / 10.6 h = **3.7/h** | 26 / 18.6 h = 1.4/h | 2.6× |
| Feature lines / h (pure moves excluded) | ~6,060 / 10.6 h ≈ 570 | 4,576 / 18.6 h ≈ 246 | 2.3× |
| Average busy lanes | ~3.0 of 8 | 2.4 of 6 | 1.25× |
| Merged before Greptile | 0 | 2 | fixed |

So speed mode gave ~2.5×, not the promised 4–5× ([speed analysis](../../research/2026-10-26-dev-speed-analysis.md) §5). The missing factor is in that analysis's own top two recommendations, which the run **did not implement**: "never block coding on review" (stacked PRs) and "lanes per area, not per unit". `dispatch.sh` starts a unit only when every dependency is `merged`, and it locks every file the unit lists (`dispatch.sh:54-68`). Every unit starts a fresh lane from cold.

## 5. What went well (evidence)

1. **The script gates held every time.** merge-gate.sh allowed 0 merges without Greptile SUCCESS on the head and 0 with an open P1 (mh1024 had 2). It enforced the size cap 3 times on Lease-R. The hook blocked all local type-checks and test runs: none appear in the 61 lane logs.
2. **Throughput was 2.6× mh1024** (§4), with 25 background deploys and no lane ever waiting on a deploy.
3. **The dispatcher ended idle gaps with startable work.** There was no "orchestrator idle" episode (it repeated in sr2, sr3 and mh1024). The idle time that remained came from the dependency graph, not from supervision.
4. **Argon caught real P0s before merge**: the Lease-R HRCEN `PROPERTY_REQUIRED` regression, a lock-token mismatch, and private files in DS1-P0. The Grok 4.7 fallback caught E1 scope creep (10:03).
5. **The timebox rule worked.** Lease-R was split at round 4 into A #562 and R2 #573. DS3-C got one redesign round. Neither went past that.
6. **The DV P0 diagnosis was thorough**: 6 causes found and fixed. P2b/P2c were proven live (canary 03580008: 9/9 terminal, 0 expired). Background prepare now fires on real DV mail (6d475807, 08:07).

## 6. What went badly (evidence)

1. **The ETA was 2.64× over**, and the right number was already in the plan (§4).
2. **Hotspot files kept the chain serial.** 7 units owned `hms-jobs.ts` and 6 owned `quote-workflow-panel.tsx` (`units.tsv`). The S0 split moved code out, but units still listed the hub files, so the dispatcher's file lock serialized them.
3. **Main was red all night**: 22–23 pane tests plus an intermittent vite TS2769 (`baseline-failures.md`). The rule "pass if failures ⊆ baseline" stayed prose until a standing exemption at 09:36Z. Until then, F2, G2a, DS2-S3 and S0-PANEL each stopped and waited for the coordinator.
4. **The coordinator (Opus) drove live DV work itself**: canaries, prod SQL, an EC2 login probe and the P0b apply. That was ~4 h of serial coordinator attention and most of the **~$82 Opus** cost. It breaks MISTAKES 10-23 ("orchestrator never drives HMS"), and the run's own `RULES.md` said the opposite ("live steps = coordinator only").
5. **The DV P0 bar is still unproven.** Canary 3 was lost to an HMS blip and to idempotency: a key from 10-23 blocked re-preparing the mail. Canary 4 has sat at DEV −10 behind staff prefill since 08:12.
6. **A planned outage was escalated as an incident** (09:12 ALERT; HRCEN down 08:00–14:00Z, ask 230). This repeats sr2 #8.
7. **Disk at 85% paused the train** (09:50). The space went to a 27 GB set-aside mastra DB, 8.2 GB of 100 web releases (never pruned), 13 GB of HMS evidence and 9 GB of docker volumes. #591–#593 waited about 2 h.
8. **Coordinator slips** (`MISTAKES-run2.md`): a units.tsv mis-edit launched F2 without its dependency; `checkout -B main` in a secondary worktree moved the deploy ref; T1 progress was misreported (25/30 instead of 22/34); canary 3 used a mail that already had an idempotency key; dv-install wrote an empty corpus file.
9. **The first Grok 4.7 fallback reviews returned bogus CHANGES** on 2 units (trust prompt). Fixed by adding `--trust` and retrying any review that has no VERDICT.

## 9. Five fixes (structural, enforced by scripts)

| # | Fix | How (enforced by) | Time saved (est.) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Only real dependencies, seam first, lanes per area** | units.tsv gets a `needs` column (API/data deps) separate from `files`. A T0 seam PR adds registry types and empty slots to the hub files, so feature units own only **new** files. `dispatch.sh` locks owned files, never hubs, and starts a dependent unit on its parent's **branch** (stacked) once the parent's gates pass, without waiting for the merge. Chains inside one area (G1→G2a→G2b, E1→E2, B1→B2) go to **one lane** that produces stacked PRs, so no link starts cold | T1 critical path drops from 11 links × 50 min to ~4 real links: **~4–5 h saved on a T1-sized run**. T2 goes from 5.5–7 h serial to **~80–120 min** ([T2 re-plan](../../plans/2026-10-26-003-t2-fast-replan.md)) | ~60 min (dispatch.sh ~30 LOC + planning template) |
| 2 | **`gate.sh`: one run per head SHA, compared to a machine baseline** | One command ships the commit, then runs type-check and the named tests, so the SKIP_TYPECHECK ordering trap goes away. Results are cached by SHA. It passes iff the failing set ⊆ `baseline.json`, which a nightly remote run on main writes. Lanes never stop on baseline failures. Add a T0 **green-main unit** that fixes or quarantines the 22 pane tests and pins the duplicate Vite types | ~60 min of critical-path stalls this run, plus ~10% of lane time | ~60 min script + 1 unit |
| 3 | **A canary lane, not the coordinator** | `canary.sh <property> <workflow>` picks a fresh mail by SQL (0 prior runs per strategy; the idempotency key includes a dead-letter epoch, so a mail can be re-prepared), enqueues at **PREFILL** priority, waits for a terminal state and writes a 5-line result. Readiness (`install-check.sh`) checks **content**: corpus not empty, ≥1 policy doc, and the letter regex scores 5 on a live sample. The coordinator only reads results | DV P0: 3 of the 6 causes found at kickoff instead of over 5 h, and the canary wait cut from 3 h+ queued to ~1 h. Opus ~$82 → ~$25 | ~90 min |
| 4 | **Infra preflight in the deploy train** | Before kickoff and before each deploy: if disk is ≥75%, prune automatically (web releases keep live + 2; evidence older than 14 d goes to S3 and is then deleted; set-aside DBs go to S3). A per-property HMS login probe runs every 10 min. If login fails in the known pattern, it **pauses that property's queue** and shows staff "HMS down", with no incident escalation. The T2-HMSDOWN and T2-DISK units are already dispatched | ~2 h deploy hold today; no false incident; no dead letters during outages | 2 T2 units (~80 min, running) |
| 5 | **`eta.sh` computes the ETA from the DAG and re-quotes on change** | It takes the longest `needs` chain × the measured per-link p50/p75 (or2: 41 / ~60 min launch→merge) plus the measured canary tail. The ledger start row must include its output. Adding a row to units.tsv prints the new ETA and logs a scope trade (PRODUCT rule 7) | Owner trust: at 01:40 it would have printed ~480–600, not 240 | ~30 min |

Order: 2 and 5 first (small, and they unblock T2 today), then 1 (planning template + dispatch), then 3. Fix 4 is already running as T2 units.
