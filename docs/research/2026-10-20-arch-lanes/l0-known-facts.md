## What this means for the agent/pane

- (inference) Model the email as current work-state plus a primary action and linked tasks; keep extraction kind, business action, payment evidence, and workflow progress separate. This preserves overlap without routing every voucher as POP or every group mail as new booking.
- (inference) For standard work, use deterministic action workflows with LLM extraction/clarification and bounded read-only HMS lookup. For open questions, return evidence and ask staff/client; never let the model choose an HMS write. Bind staff approval to the exact prepared mutation, then verify by read-back. Keep mail unsent for staff approval; there is no Nava send path in scope. [HITL mail boundary](/home/ubuntu/projects/zero/.worktrees/oct18-c1-classify/docs/infor-hms/centurion-email-tool-demand-handoff-2026-08-03.md:80) [Write approval](/home/ubuntu/projects/zero/.worktrees/oct18-c1-classify/docs/plans/2026-10-14-002-one-click-case-card-plan.md:3)
- (inference) Keep quote and new booking together until the staff/owner policy says which end state applies. Prefetch dates, occupancy, company and agency candidates, current rate authority, availability and price; show all alternatives with their distinguishing HMS fields.
- (inference) Pane order should start with mailbox/thread context and current action, then party/contact and rate authority, show availability plus rates before staff choose room type, then stay details/extras/missing-info, evidence/documents, and one primary action. Adjust exact order only after direct Infor screen research.
- (inference) Payment should be explicit evidence states: provisional/deposit zero, deposit or folio evidence when an authorized read exposes it, POP attached but unmatched, agent voucher, guarantee-only, unknown. Do not collapse guarantee, voucher, POP, and posted money into a paid boolean.
- (inference) Capture staff edits/picks as eval labels after confirming which source is truth; do not treat conversational memory as pricing or policy authority.

## Open gaps

- Final action schema: define needs-action vs action, primary label vs secondary tasks, system/TMC notice handling, existing-booking voucher behavior, and when group/event overrides a room action.
- Canonical group boundary: owner guest threshold, historical room-count/event routes, and line-quantity heuristic need one explicit owner-approved rule.
- Quote-to-book decision: when to create provisional HMS booking versus send a hand quote PDF; whether HRCEN_Offer is an offer without a reservation; how to reconcile the conflicting HRCEN count.
- Document lifecycle: exact HMS create/save/Confirm-to-Email clicks for this tenant; how HRCEN_Offer is generated/sent; whether separate pro-forma print/send is still common; source and ownership of the hand-named pro-formas and Excel files.
- Payment truth: per-booking source of guarantee, deposit paid/due, folio and matched POP; whether an agent voucher counts as prepaid for that stay; how later postings change the provisional letter.
- Paid amend/cancel policy: cost/rate changes for date/pax/room changes; partial room/night treatment; release versus cancel; fee/forfeit/refund; waiver authority; required checks and confirmation.
- Infor creation sequence: live field-by-field screen order, required vs optional staff fields, profile selectors, rate grid, availability grid, and behavior of guarantee/settlement controls.
- Reader completeness: preserve party location/IATA/reference/id and nightly rates/stock through reader → stored projection → pane; confirm whether deployed code still drops them.
- Party truth: choose quote staff pick vs reservation party precedence; prove current sender-history writer and deployment; measure right/abstain/wrong on independent, current mails.
- Ask-back policy: count real missing-info questions and answers by date, occupancy, room-share, child age, board, group, and party; current R1 shapes are not staff-question labels.
