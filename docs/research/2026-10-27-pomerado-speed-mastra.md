## Model cost and counterevidence

OpenAI's Standard short-context list prices, dollars per million input/output: Luna **$0.10 / $0.50**; Terra **$1.00 / $5.00**. Cached input: Luna **$0.01**, Terra **$0.05**. [Official pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing); [Microsoft's Luna Azure Global Standard rates](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/gpt-6-astra-sol-and-luna-for-production-agents-in-microsoft-foundry/) match those short-context Luna figures. Azure contract, region, tier, cache writes, extra retries and full per-role use are unknown, so the actual cost per successful task is unknown.

Luna max may be cheaper per unit, not necessarily faster or more reliable. [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/comparisons/gpt-6-1-terra-xhigh-vs-gpt-6-luna) reports Terra xhigh 46% versus Luna max 13% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, while SciCode is 53% versus 55%. Its measured task times also favor Terra on its own evaluation. These are independent but not Pomerado/HMS trials. They justify watching the first outcome, not rejecting the owner's Luna choice.

## Evidence threshold

**Met** for what the vendor says it builds, generic Mastra MCP support, the locally inspected Nava paths, the price-list comparison and the correction of the old ETA's scope. **Not met** for HRCEN publication/accuracy, time to success, Luna-max effective routing, direct drop-in compatibility, relative speed/cost versus Nava, or total stack replacement. Needed: a real selected-record mint and independent replay, then the preserved three-case comparison and same-task Nava baseline. Route: local source and existing reports plus Exa basic search and primary-page fetch. No Exa Agent or subagent was used. This research's Fable/model and search charges are not available here.
