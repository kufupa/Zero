## Older staff quote source follow-up

A separate source review found that [`runStaffQuote`](../../../../apps/server-pilot/src/ai/staff-quote-run.ts#L611-L685) scopes an inbound message and property, prepares an HMS quote and re-checks staff-picked parties when rate context is enabled. When that switch is off, it removes parties and records a note rather than silently binding them. The [rate option type](../../../../apps/server-pilot/src/ai/rate-options.ts#L25-L49) distinguishes room-only, bed-and-breakfast and dinner-inclusive options. [Quote revisions](../../../../apps/server-pilot/src/quote-proposal/quote-revision.ts#L119-L133) distinguish observed availability from held inventory. A [PDF job path](../../../../apps/server-pilot/src/ai/quote-pdf-jobs.ts#L113-L161) also exists.

The pane's [auto-start hook](../../../../apps/mail/components/nava-pane/use-staff-quote-autostart.ts#L70-L128) already attempts a quote when its guards pass. Among other conditions, it requires quote intent, a signed-in user, queue mode on, the matching selected message, a ready source, and no party-lookup wait. The [auto-start binding](../../../../apps/mail/lib/quote-workflow/staff-quote-bind.ts#L1428-L1453) queues a persisted staff quote when request construction and queue dependencies are available; it does not fall back to inline HMS. [Quote preparation](../../../../apps/server-pilot/src/ai/staff-quote-workflow.ts#L361-L469) rejects groups with different stay dates and declares PMS write and email send capabilities false. These are code-level pieces, not proof of end-to-end pricing, the current switch, or automatic quote readiness on either staff surface.

That check did not inspect effective in-process values, all flag sources, or a live quote. The checked-in `hms_find_reservation` digest remains registry evidence, not a current property canary.
