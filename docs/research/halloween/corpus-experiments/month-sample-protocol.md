# Month-sample protocol

**Purpose:** Test whether the additional saved mail changes the quote-first slice. The older case reviews and confirmation-anchored examples reveal failure modes, but they cannot establish what a typical request contains.

## Predeclared small check

1. From the saved Centurion snapshot, identify inbound logical message records dated 28 September–23 October 2026. Respect the source's active-transport and logical-message definitions. Record duplicate and window limitations; a saved row is not proof of delivery.
2. Split messages into a broad quote-like text-candidate stratum and its complement using a declared, intentionally inclusive local rule. This is a triage aid, not a classifier or an accuracy claim.
3. Draw a reproducible fixed-seed sample across four stored-date blocks: 28 Sep–4 Oct, 5–11 Oct, 12–18 Oct and 19–23 Oct. Aim for ten candidate and five complement messages per block if available. Rank source rows by SHA-256 of a fixed seed and the private source-row ID. Review the first stratum for quote handling and use the complement as a small search for missed cases. Report actual counts, inclusion rules, missing data and all abstentions. The strata are intentionally oversampled and are not a representative estimate of overall case mix.
4. First review only information available at the selected incoming message, including earlier same-thread material only when actually present at that point. For each relevant human quote request, record dates, room count, occupancy, board, company/agency clues, whether a quoted price can be known, and which facts remain unclear. Do not fill missing fields from later replies, PDFs or HMS.
5. Only after recording the input assessment, inspect later saved same-thread responses as separate outcome evidence. Categorize the first supported staff action as price/quote, clarification, management/manual approval, decline/alternative, other, or no saved response. A no-response finding is bounded to saved rows and does not establish no reply elsewhere.
6. Independently recheck a small subset of labels and source pointers. Differences and ambiguous cases stay visible. Use rates and conclusions only where their evidence permits; an email price is not independent current HMS pricing, and claimed attachments are not verified files.

The sample informs specific product requirements and where to abstain. It is not a golden set, a production pass, a validated recall/precision estimate, or a reason to change the owner-set Quote → Create → Amend order. Exact source pointers and any message excerpts stay in a new owner-only ignored directory. Published notes contain only anonymized summaries, aggregate counts and reproducible method.

For an internal rerun against the unchanged saved snapshot, pass the source listed in [the inventory](month-source-inventory.md#scope-and-method) and a **new** direct child of `.private` to `python3 -I -B docs/research/halloween/corpus-experiments/month-sample-select.py SOURCE_JSONL NEW_PRIVATE_DIR`. The selector refuses an existing output directory, fingerprints the source on both passes, and writes its packets and manifest owner-only. The warning files described below were generated separately from saved source metadata; they are not produced by the selector.

**Selection:** The protocol was recorded before selection and review. The fixed-seed selector independently counted 5,229 saved in-window messages in 3,364 threads, including 3,843 inbound and 1,386 outbound. It selected 60 inbound messages from 59 threads: 40 text candidates and 20 complement records, with the intended 10/5 per date block. The saved-source inventory independently reported the same total and thread counts. The text rule, exact seed, scoping and source fingerprint are recorded by [the selector](month-sample-select.py); the owner-only packet manifest records the draw. Logical rows are retained without unresolved deduplication.

Reviewers recorded input labels before opening the outcome packets. After outcome review, reviewer A corrected two input field-source statuses, reporting that the correction used input evidence only. The current A label file is newer than its outcome-label file. No separate original input-label snapshot was retained; the reviewer says the original values remain in the private session history. The corrected party/rate source and occupancy cells are not independently blinded and should not support strong quantitative claims.

Review packets include at most three strictly earlier same-thread messages and at most five strictly later outbound messages, all inside the date window. They mask addresses, links and long numbers and withhold text matching selected sensitive-content markers. This is a precaution, not a comprehensive de-identification guarantee. Five selected bodies were withheld and one was truncated; six samples had omitted earlier context. In five selected source messages, the long-number mask overlaps a numeric date-like string, which is not necessarily a requested stay date. A private, per-case warning flags this possibility. Attachment inventory and stored-timestamp timezone remain unknown. Case reviews must mark affected fields unknown, not use absence as proof. Two selected messages share a thread, so observations are not fully independent.

**Status:** An independent replay reproduced all 60 selected source rows, the source fingerprint, and packet chronology. A separate source-metadata check found an intervening inbound message before the first later outbound for three sampled selections. A later outbound is not automatically a response to the selected message. Private outcome-only warnings retain this context without leaking it into the input labels. An independent reviewer assessed eight fixed cases, one from each date-block/stratum combination, and froze their input labels before seeing outcome packets. They and the first reviewers agreed on six of eight input classifications and six of eight later-action categories. The two classification disagreements were `unclear` versus `no`; among two cases where both considered input quote-relevant or unclear, one also differed on occupancy and board. A follow-up assessment characterized the board evidence as mixed and occupancy as unstated; that clarification was made after the reviewer had seen the outcome packets, so it was not blind. Keep the original uncertain cases visible. These are small qualitative checks, not validated accuracy estimates. Two additional purposively selected cases were requested to check whether earlier context adds a fact or only changes thread interpretation. They were not blind-reviewed: the reviewer treated the original eight-case delegation as a fixed user privacy boundary and declined the expansion. Their original labels remain provisional; no independent check is claimed. The completed [product synthesis](month-decision.md) retains these limits.
