# Centurion corpus analysis: counterfactual-help method

**Purpose:** a repeatable, evidence-first method for estimating what Nava could have helped with on historical Centurion requests under a named counterfactual capability scenario. The primary scenario requested here is: **if the capabilities explicitly described in the November 1 plan had existed at the historical decision point, what could they have helped with?** Score only documented planned scope and its assumptions; do not require runtime validation to answer that counterfactual, and do not call a planned tool live or ready. Keep implementation evidence separate. A second, optional evidence-proven-runtime scenario may be reported independently for an identified property/version. This is a method note, not a corpus result, production evaluation, usage report, or authorization to access mail or live systems.

The target is counterfactual historical assistance—not observed staff usage. Product guidance records that staff were not using Nava because it was incomplete. A staff reply or HMS outcome can be separate evidence, but neither is a measure of Nava adoption or a substitute for the assistance label. [Product state and human approval](../../../../PRODUCT.md#L9-L11)

**Study:** [250-email Centurion pilot plan](2026-11-01-centurion-250-pilot-plan.md), [execution record](2026-11-01-pilot-execution.md), and [results](2026-11-01-centurion-250-pilot-results.md). The [newer Box 1 plan](../../../plans/2026-11-01-002-hms-cloud-create-e2e-box-plan.md#8-after-box-1-and-what-is-out) supersedes the original plan's native two-room pilot: Box 2 instead proposes a loop of individual creates without native linking.

## 1. Estimand and unit

At the selected inbound decision point, ask: **given only evidence then available, what would the named scenario have helped staff prepare, find, compare, or safely review?** For the primary counterfactual, the scenario is the November 1 plan as written, including its stated limits—not every feature that might someday be built. A possible write remains staff-approved; a guest-facing draft remains unsent. `Complete` does not mean autonomous booking, send, payment, or fulfillment.

Use **request episode × stay × requested action** as the review unit. Keep source-row, message, thread, mailbox, and attachment counts as separate upstream denominators. One mail may refer to several stays; one stay may be discussed across messages; a repeated/cross-mailbox copy is not automatically a new request. Keep private linkage keys outside Git. [Existing unit guidance](../2026-10-31-hms-inbound-automation-baseline.md#L20-L28) [Multi-stay example and limits](../corpus-experiments/month-stay-cases.md#L7-L13)

Assign one assistance label per unit, with evidence and a reason:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `complete` | Under the declared scenario, every material part of the requested help could be prepared; required target, facts, and next step are supported, with no material unresolved ambiguity. For a planned-capability counterfactual, state that assumption explicitly. Any write still requires staff approval and its own safe proof/readback. |
| `partial` | Under the declared scenario, at least one material part could be usefully prepared, but a material field, target, price, relationship, document, or fulfillment step still needs staff clarification, manual work, or a capability outside the scenario. State the gap. |
| `no_material_help` | The request is clearly in scope, but the declared scenario contains no capability that would materially help at that decision point. Do not use this for missing evidence. |
| `unknown` | Input, source coverage, intent, target, or capability evidence is too incomplete or conflicting to decide. Preserve why. |
| `out_of_scope` | The request is clear but excluded by the current owner scope/policy. Keep the reason separate from origin and intent. |

For each material part, record whether it is included in the declared counterfactual and separately record implementation evidence: `validated_runtime`, `validated_component_only`, `code_or_test_only`, `planned`, `not_supported`, or `unknown`, with property, code/model version, and evidence date. A planned capability may support a primary *hypothetical* label only when the plan specifies it; it never proves current runtime or staff availability. A tool name, source file, test, plan-readiness label, merge, or historical Labtest case does not prove current runtime. The November 1 plan is a plan; its verification contract separates tool proof from end-user product completion. [Plan authority and done boundary](../../../plans/2026-11-01-001-feat-hms-cloud-modular-tools-plan.md#L14-L30) [Tool/runtime evidence limits](../2026-11-01-hms-17h-retro.md#L13-L21) [Plan verification](../../../plans/2026-11-01-001-feat-hms-cloud-modular-tools-plan.md#L281-L303)

## 2. Evidence-first, multi-axis labels

Do not compress origin, intent, scope, staff behavior, and hypothetical help into one classifier label. Store evidence/source spans and confidence for each axis; multi-label asks are allowed.

| Axis | Record | Keep separate from |
|---|---|---|
| Source and origin | Mailbox/transport, direction, stored time, sender/channel clues, explicit OTA/system/automation flag, `no-reply` clue, and evidence/confidence for each. | Request/action. A host, domain, HMS source-of-business value, or no-reply clue does not authenticate a sender, establish intent, or prove automation. |
| Request and action asked | Current authored ask, quoted/forwarded material, one or more request classes, and source spans. Distinguish quote, new booking, existing-stay amendment, person-requested cancellation, billing, proof of payment, other payment work, voucher/document, service/transfer, group/event, other work, no current ask, marketing/spam, and unclear. | Origin, later staff response, and whether work completed. |
| Target and requirements | Property; stay/guest/party candidates and match basis; requested dates, explicit room count/type, occupancy/children, board/rate, add-ons, billing/payment facts, and contradictions/unknowns. Preserve one unit per stay/action where needed. | A candidate match is not a binding; a later HMS value is not the historical request. |
| Scope/policy | `in_scope`, `out_of_scope`, or `policy_conflict`, plus the exact rule/evidence. | Semantic intent: a skipped channel message may still contain a discernible request, which should not be rewritten as `no_action`. |
| Observed outcome | Staff response/action, independent record/document evidence, and outcome confidence/coverage. Keep no-reply-in-snapshot distinct from proof that no reply occurred. | Counterfactual assistance. Later text or a changed PDF alone does not prove an HMS write, payment, or fulfillment. |
| Potential Nava help | One of the five labels above, tied to a frozen capability version and the information available at that decision point. | Observed staff use, message volume, or model consensus. |

Keep **billing**, **payment**, and **voucher** distinct. Billing includes invoice/folio/account or billing-instruction work; payment includes a claim/proof, refund, or transaction/payment state; a voucher is its own document/workflow signal. A voucher alone is not proof that funds cleared, and a request for an invoice is not evidence of payment. Preserve payment *claim*, attached proof, and independently verified posted/cleared funds as different states. [Owner scope and existing finance limits](../2026-11-01-hms-tooling-parked-items.md#L19-L24) [Product finance/tooling plan](../../../plans/2026-11-01-001-feat-hms-cloud-modular-tools-plan.md#L65-L78)

**Owner clarification, 2026-11-01:** a supplied proof of payment or voucher should be treated as document-filing work for the relevant reservation, in addition to any booking, billing or payment action. For analysis, keep `proof of payment` and `voucher` as distinct labels and record the common filing task separately:

1. Distinguish a sender's claim that a file is attached from verified accessible file bytes; missing attachment evidence stays unknown. A receipt may be an image, not a PDF.
2. Find the correct property and existing stay; retain ambiguous or missing matches for staff. If the email also requests a new booking and no stay exists, the filing task follows the separately approved booking step; a voucher alone does not prove that a stay must be created. Check for an already-filed document, but loaded filename/metadata alone cannot prove identical file bytes or a complete document list.
3. Prepare the correct provided file for the stay's HMS Documents area; require staff approval for the upload and report what a later readback actually verifies. Do not silently choose an unrelated file or claim attachment from metadata alone.
4. For proof of payment, separately check whether the claimed payment is recorded or cleared; filing a receipt does not prove that. For a voucher, review its booking/billing instructions without assuming a new booking or settled payment. Groups over 10 rooms remain outside the current group-work scope.

Treat document filing as a requested action unit for the assistance metric. In-scope material help with filing can contribute to an email's any-help numerator, even if it is the only relevant task; do not claim a file is available when its evidence is missing. Track unique files separately, so one file serving both POP and voucher purposes is not counted twice. If the same file must be filed against separate stays, those are separate stay-level filing tasks.

The November 1 plan includes a **generic approved-document attachment capability**, while proof-of-payment extraction/OCR, intake triage and an end-to-end POP/voucher filing workflow remain parked or unverified. A planned uploader may help with the filing subtask; its existence does not establish that the whole workflow is deployed. [Current scope](../2026-11-01-hms-tooling-parked-items.md#ocr-pop-extraction-and-intake-triage) [Planned Cloud document capability](../../../plans/2026-11-01-001-feat-hms-cloud-modular-tools-plan.md#u7-finance-and-generic-document-attachment) [POP examples and limits](../2026-10-31-hms-coverage-run.md#follow-up-payment-proof-vs-billing-31-oct)

For a simple additive dashboard chart, a **proposed display roll-up** can use one label per inbound message: multiple work types; group request over 10 rooms; quote; new booking; amendment; cancellation; billing; proof of payment; other payment work; voucher; other work; informational/no current work; marketing/spam; or unknown. For a message with several substantive work types, choose `multiple work types` before the other rows. The filing step inherent in a POP or voucher does not alone make that message mixed. For a single group request over 10 rooms, choose the group row while retaining its quote/create/etc. intent underneath. Keep a separate group tag so group work inside a mixed message is still countable. OTA/channel/automation is an overlapping source flag, not a substitute for work type. This is a research/reporting proposal, not a change to current product routing.

## 3. Reproducible study protocol

1. **Freeze scope before selection.** Record owner-authorized purpose, property, mailbox/transport IDs, saved-source manifest/fingerprint, snapshot cutoff, date window/timezone basis, direction, retention, and known missing sources/attachments. This README authorizes no fresh mailbox query or live call.
2. **Census the frame.** Count source rows, logical messages, threads, direction, mailbox/transport and date; record mixed-scope rows, duplicate candidates, cross-mailbox overlap, raw-MIME/attachment coverage, and outcome-source coverage. A saved row is not proof of delivery. Retain source rows until duplicate delivery is independently established; message IDs/fingerprints alone are candidate links, not safe deduplication. [Centurion source method and limits](../corpus-experiments/month-source-inventory.md#L5-L12) [Duplicate/attachment limits](../corpus-experiments/month-source-inventory.md#L54-L68)
3. **Declare the unit and inclusion rules.** Start with inbound source rows in a named scope/window; preserve automated/channel, no-action, unanswered, declined, withdrawn, and no-pattern cases in the frame. After review, cluster verified copies into request episodes and split multi-stay/multi-action requests into request × stay × action units. Keep unresolved linkage as candidate/unknown, not a merge. A broad text rule may find candidates, but candidate/complement strata are discovery aids, not labels.
4. **Predeclare sample design.** If a full model assessment is infeasible, stratify across stored-time blocks and decision-relevant sources/classes, including a randomly selected complement to catch missed asks. Rank by a recorded fixed seed plus private source-row ID, with deterministic tie breaks; retain population and selected counts/inclusion probabilities in an owner-only manifest. Oversampling rare/candidate strata requires design weights for population estimates. Purposive anchors and unweighted oversamples support case discovery only. Reuse the past seed/code only when its source snapshot and scope match. [Historical predeclared sample](../corpus-experiments/month-sample-protocol.md#L5-L18)
5. **Freeze decision-time inputs and protect them.** Give models only the selected authored request and relevant context genuinely available by that point; label quoted/forwarded authorship and earlier-message omissions. A stored timestamp alone may be a sender `Date` value and can make a reply appear earlier than its target; verify arrival ordering and message ancestry, and omit separately sourced context whose ordering remains unclear. Quoted content already in the target remains available, with authorship caveats. Include an attachment only when its bytes/content and provenance are verified. Keep later replies, PDFs, HMS reads, and payment outcomes in a separate sealed outcome packet. Guest/private mail must remain in a permitted privacy-preserving processing route; never send raw or masked mail to web tools or remote agents. If no permitted route exists for the intended model, do not run it and leave feasibility unassessed. Existing masks are lossy and are not complete de-identification. Mark redacted, withheld, truncated, missing, or out-of-window evidence as unknown; do not infer absence from an incomplete export. [Leak-free evaluation rule](../corpus-experiments/decision.md#L45-L50) [Timestamp/reply caveat](../../../../apps/server-pilot/scripts/triage-dataset/build-prompts.ts#L63-L72) [Existing privacy and packet limits](../corpus-experiments/month-sample-protocol.md#L20-L23) [Product privacy boundary](../../../../PRODUCT.md#L43)
6. **Use a model-led, multi-axis label protocol.** Because large-scale human labeling is unavailable, obtain two independent, blinded model assessments of each decision-time input packet, preferably from distinct permitted models; record exact model/prompt versions, source spans, confidence, and abstentions. Count a provisional `complete`/`partial`/`no_material_help` label only when both agree on the class and cite compatible input evidence plus the same named-scenario capability mapping. Treat disagreement or abstention as `unknown`; do not manufacture consensus. If only one model can be used, call a second run a repeated assessment—not independent-model agreement. Repeat a predeclared same-packet subset or arm to estimate model variability; agreement is stability, not truth. After input/help labels are frozen, assess the sealed outcome packet separately; use bounded, targeted review against independent saved outcome evidence for outcome disagreements only. Never let later outcomes revise decision-time intent or counterfactual-help labels. If an outcome claim cannot be independently resolved, or an input/help disagreement remains unresolved, retain `unknown`. Do not assume owner/human review of the full set. The separate front-door policy remains staff outbound first, otherwise two models agreeing, with disagreements sent to the owner when that workflow is applicable; this policy is not the counterfactual-help metric, and unavailable adjudication must not be replaced by assumed consensus. [Current front-door policy](../../../context/PRODUCT-DETAIL.md#L5-L7)
7. **Establish outcomes independently.** After input labels freeze, record staff response/action separately from exact-stay HMS state/change, attachment/document evidence, and actual payment/ledger evidence. Use the best independent source available; mark absent/late/partial evidence unknown. Later HMS captures can verify a later read, not reconstruct the historical state at email time; PDFs and staff words alone do not establish a save. [Outcome sequence and limits](../2026-10-31-hms-inbound-automation-baseline.md#L22-L28) [Historical read boundary](../corpus-experiments/month-stay-cases.md#L31-L48)
8. **Map to a frozen capability scenario, then score help.** Map each claimed assistable part to an explicit planned operation and assumption, preserving the current individual-create loop and parked native linking/group/XLSX work. Score this counterfactual even where runtime is not validated, but label implementation evidence separately and never imply the planned capability is live. If useful, report a second evidence-proven-runtime scenario for the named property/version; do not merge its counts with the primary scenario.
9. **Measure reviewer/model noise.** On the same frozen decision-time packets, record per-axis agreement, unknowns, confidence, and disagreement reasons. Repeat an unchanged model/reviewer arm to measure baseline noise before attributing label changes to context or a new prompt/tool. In one targeted Oct 31 same-packet repeat, primary labels changed on 4/24 cases and action sets on 5/24; this demonstrates reviewer/inference variability in that small experiment, not a production error rate. Compare versions on the same versioned rows; show row changes separately. Agreement measures stability, not correctness; use independent evidence/adjudication for correctness. [Context-arm method](../corpus-experiments/compare-context-arms.py#L71-L98) [Targeted repeat result and caveat](../2026-10-31-hms-coverage-run.md#L578-L581)
10. **Publish only sanitized aggregates.** Keep source maps, row IDs, excerpts, model transcripts, attachments, and any identifying material private. Publish scope, method, numerator/denominators, missingness, uncertainty, source/version, exclusions, and disagreement counts—never raw mail, guest identifiers, addresses, credentials, or private outputs.

The owner is open to a large LLM analysis. To reduce repeated input while preserving meaning, remove clearly identified branding/signature graphics and repeated boilerplate, but keep receipt images, business files and ambiguous attachments. De-duplicate repeated quoted blocks without dropping relevant history: one reviewed reminder had its substantive request in the quoted context. Retain the selected current message, relevant earlier context, sender/channel clues, dates, room counts, references and document provenance; never leak a later reply into the decision-time packet. Preserve a private link to the original and mark anything removed, unreadable or uncertain. Existing masking is not a complete de-identification guarantee. An older corpus hydrator treats any inline or Content-ID image as decorative; do not reuse that blanket rule without checking for receipts. This is a source-level risk, not evidence that a particular receipt was lost. ## 4. Denominators and reporting

Report a reconciled funnel, not one floating percentage:

- saved source rows by transport/mailbox/window/direction;
- logical messages and threads (not interchangeable with source rows);
- candidate duplicate clusters, confirmed copy clusters, and unresolved overlaps;
- sampled messages, reviewed request episodes, and request × stay × action units;
- units in scope, out of scope by reason, and unknown/policy-conflict;
- for each specified capability scenario: `complete`, `partial`, `no_material_help`, and `unknown`, plus observed-outcome evidence coverage and implementation-evidence status.
- **All-inbound message-level numerator/denominator:** distinct selected inbound logical-message records in the frozen frame linked to at least one `complete` or `partial` request × stay × action unit, including filing / all selected inbound logical-message records in that frame, including no-current-ask, out-of-scope, and unknown messages. Count each selected record once; unresolved copies remain, so these are not verified distinct delivered emails. This is an any-help message incidence, not the in-scope task-level rate.
- Report proof-of-payment and voucher messages separately from file-level filing tasks. One email can contain both or contain other reservation work; count it once in message-level denominators, while preserving each distinct document and its available/missing, matched/ambiguous and filed/unknown status. One file serving both purposes must not become two unique files.

Do not silently exclude unknowns. For each named scenario, if an inferential sample is justified, report a resolved-only descriptive share over in-scope request × stay × action units `(complete + partial) / (complete + partial + no_material_help)` **with** unknown/out-of-scope counts, and an uncertainty interval. Also show bounds over potentially in-scope units: lower `(complete + partial) / (complete + partial + no_material_help + unknown)`; upper `(complete + partial + unknown) / (complete + partial + no_material_help + unknown)`. Keep out-of-scope counts visible outside this denominator. These bounds handle labels left unknown only; they do not bound label errors, missing frame coverage, linkage errors, or scenario-mapping error. Apply inclusion weights and cluster-aware uncertainty only when the sample design supports them; otherwise report counts/case findings, not prevalence. Multilabel axes can overlap, so their totals need not sum.

There is no supported Centurion counterfactual-help percentage or pass bar in this note. The prior 60-message sample was stratified/oversampled and exploratory; its labels focus on quote relevance and field-source/status, not the current help scenario or a structured `>10 requested rooms` decision. The Oct 31 125-case discovery index includes room-related request/field tags but no normalized numeric room quantity or shared tool-success/capability-support label. Neither sample yields the excluded-group rate or planned-help percentage. The older R3 weighted estimate measured historical staff-action need, not Nava assistance; its 400-row labels cover action/needs-action, free-text evidence, and overlapping agent-notice/booking-request/POP-evidence/voucher flags. A coordinator's 2026-11-01 aggregate-only schema inspection found no structured requested-room count, capability-version support, or human-approval fields, so these labels cannot be mechanically converted to the current metric. Bounds above account for units labeled `unknown` only; they do not bound label errors, missing frame coverage, linkage errors, or incorrect scenario mapping.
