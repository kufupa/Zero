# Centurion 250-email corpus pilot

**Goal:** test a repeatable way to estimate what Nava **could have helped with** in historical reservation work, under the November 1 planned capabilities. Produce a research result that can inform a later dashboard and be adapted for Da Vinci. This is not a measurement of staff adoption, a claim that the tools are live, or a dashboard implementation.

**Status:** this document is the plan. No messages have been selected or classified for this pilot. The owner requested 250 messages, extensive model-led review, and GPT-6 Luna Max subagents; model expense is not the limiting factor. Research uses the saved corpus, not a new mailbox or HMS query.

## 1. What the result needs to say

For each inbound decision point, identify the work requested, where it came from, whether it is in scope, and what the frozen tool plan could materially help staff prepare. Assess each distinct **request × stay × action**; then roll up to messages without counting the same email twice. Preserve mixed requests instead of forcing all meaning into one label.

| Axis | Record |
|---|---|
| Origin | Channel/OTA/system and automated clues, travel agent, direct or internal, with evidence and uncertainty. Origin is not the requested work or proof of sender authenticity. |
| Work | Quote, new booking, amendment, person-requested cancellation, cancellation notification, billing, proof of payment, other payment, voucher, service, group/event, other, no current work, or unknown; more than one can apply. A TMC cancellation notification needs no action under current owner policy; preserve any separately evidenced work and origin. |
| Scope | In scope, explicitly excluded, unknown, or current policy conflict. A group of **more than 10 requested rooms** is excluded; exactly 10 is not. Do not infer rooms from guests. Group/event work otherwise parked in the plan is distinct from evidence for the `>10 rooms` count. |
| Document task | POP and voucher are distinct signals. Both may require finding the correct stay and filing the supplied document after staff approval. Record a claimed file separately from accessible file bytes and from confirmed payment. One file can serve more than one purpose. |
| Planned assistance | `complete`, `partial`, `no_material_help`, `unknown`, or `out_of_scope`, with supporting input spans and the relevant capability/assumption from the frozen plan. `Complete` means material preparation is covered, not that a guest request is autonomously fulfilled. Writes require staff approval; Nava does not send. |
| Staff outcome | Later replies and other saved outcome evidence, analyzed separately after decision-time labels freeze. A reply is not Nava adoption or proof of an HMS write. |

Map assistance against the [November 1 Cloud plan](../../../plans/2026-11-01-001-feat-hms-cloud-modular-tools-plan.md#product-contract), including its bounded two-room pilot. Do not promote that pilot to support for every 2–10-room request. Generic approved-document upload is planned; POP extraction/OCR, intake wiring, group/XLSX, payment posting and cancellation commits are parked. Missing stay/profile/price/file evidence cannot be filled in by assuming a planned read will succeed. [Document scope](../2026-11-01-hms-tooling-parked-items.md#ocr-pop-extraction-and-intake-triage)

| Request | Planned capability to consider | Material boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Quote | U2 party/identity, U3 availability/pricing and U9 composition | Criteria, current rates and coverage may be unknown; the reply is unsent. |
| Create | U2 existing-profile selection and U4 staff-approved create | Missing or ambiguous profile/rate details remain unresolved. |
| Amend | U2 exact-stay lookup and U5 supported, non-held stay edits | Shared-profile changes and the specifically held room-amend probe are excluded. |
| Cancel | U2 lookup and context | Cancellation commit is parked; lookup alone is at most partial help. |
| Billing/payment | U7 finance context and supported stay-owned instructions | Do not claim automatic payment posting or a verified cleared balance. |
| POP/voucher | U2 stay lookup and U7 approved generic document attachment | File presence/identity and match may be unknown; OCR/intake is parked. |
| Multi-room | U8 bounded two-room pilot | Group/XLSX work and requests over 10 rooms are excluded; other sizes are not automatically supported. |

Under the owner's rule, an incoming proof-of-payment or voucher document supplied or explicitly claimed for a reservation creates a **document-filing action unit**, even without a literal request to file it. The action can be clearly in scope even when file bytes are missing; file availability and the help label may still be unknown. Material help with that unit can contribute to the message's any-help numerator, even if the message asks for nothing else. Keep unique-file counts separately: one file carrying both POP and voucher signals is one file and one filing task **per relevant stay**, while another booking or billing action remains its own task.

Apply the `>10` rule to the requested room total for the **same group/request episode**, not the sum of unrelated bookings in a message. Use an explicit total or sum clearly non-overlapping room allocations for that request; if the relationship or total is ambiguous, leave the threshold unknown. Apply an exclusion to the qualifying group work while retaining separate unrelated in-scope actions.

For simple reporting, show one proposed primary work row per message, using `multiple work types` for multiple substantive actions, plus overlapping origin, group and document-task flags. A filing substep inherent in a POP/voucher does not by itself create a mixed-work label. A mixed message with group work retains that group flag. This is a research taxonomy, not a product-routing change. [Existing taxonomy and policy conflicts](README.md#2-evidence-first-multi-axis-labels)

Keep message-level `any_help` and `fully_preparable` as separate yes/no/unknown roll-ups; `complete` and `partial` remain task-level labels. A message is fully preparable only if **every** material task is in scope and `complete`; a complete filing subtask does not make an email with another unresolved or excluded task fully preparable. Staff approval and any necessary sending still remain with staff.

## 2. Frame and sample

The saved Centurion source contains **11,211 inbound and 4,830 outbound source rows** dated 27 July–29 October 2026. These are saved rows, not proof of complete delivery; the snapshot has 1,171 unresolved duplicate candidates and an unverified timestamp timezone. The pilot covers inbound decision points; retain outbound totals for context and assess outbound work separately later. [Source census](centurion-origin-census.md#scope-and-counts)

| Cohort | Count | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Random development | 100 | See ordinary mail and develop the cleanup and labels. |
| Random holdout | 50 | Check the frozen approach on messages not used to tune it. |
| Targeted development | 100 | Stress-test rarer or difficult cases. |
| **Total** | **250** | Source rows, not 250 proven distinct requests or threads. |

1. Verify the owner-only manifest and the full saved JSONL fingerprint `ec9d30c47e2ef0a3dd61ae6db795e1f730feb5e853b9cf76025f523d7a4e6625`. Select `recordType=thread`, each message's own stored timestamp, `active_transport`, Centurion transport and inbound direction. Preserve private source-row and thread links. Stop and re-scope if fingerprint, membership or source schema differs. [Exact selection fields](../corpus-experiments/month-source-inventory.md#scope-and-method)
2. Before seeing contents, draw 150 from the full saved inbound frame by ranking `SHA-256(UTF-8(seed + NUL + domain + NUL + source-row ID))`, where seed is `centurion-pilot-250-v1-20261101` and domain is `random`. Use the same formula with domain `holdout` to rank those 150 and reserve the first 50; use timestamp and source-row ID as deterministic tie-breaks. The remaining 100 are random development. Record the encoding, inclusion rules, source version and actual counts in the private manifest.
3. Select 100 additional distinct development rows using broad cues in this fixed order: 20 each for OTA/system, POP, voucher, group/rooming, and mixed-action candidates. Use case-insensitive subject/body clues, the saved sender host and no-reply clue: channel hosts or integration-failure subjects; POP/proof-of-payment/remittance/EFT/payment-receipt wording; voucher wording; group/rooming/conference wording or at least 10 explicitly numbered rooms; and hits from at least two broad quote, booking, amend, cancel, billing/payment or document cue families. Where the older export below can be linked to the same target message, its attachment metadata or extracted text may also supply a **candidate-selection cue**, with observation-time limitations recorded; later staff replies and HMS outcomes must not supply cues. Do not expose the sampling stratum or cue labels to the assessing models. Freeze exact patterns before selection. For each stratum in order, rank eligible unselected rows with the same hash formula and domain `target:<stratum>`, taking up to 20; retain all overlapping cue flags. Fill shortfalls deterministically from remaining candidate strata in the same order, then from the general non-holdout frame with domain `fallback`, recording each backfill. Exclude holdout rows and linked threads before calculating targeted cues; do not inspect holdout contents or their cue flags until freeze. Keyword/host hits are candidate finders, not proven classifications; non-matching random rows help find misses.
4. Keep the 50 holdout rows and their outcomes sealed until the cleaner, prompts and capability mapping freeze. Exclude their threads and plausible duplicate-linked records from targeted development. If a nominal random-development row shares a holdout thread or credible duplicate link, retain it in the 250 but embargo it until after freeze; report the actual development count. Flag ambiguous cross-thread copies, and never claim body equality or a fingerprint alone proves duplicate delivery. Record overlap with older reviewed cases when verifiable; previous research means the holdout is not a globally unseen gold set.
5. Keep random and targeted results separate. The 250 combined are deliberately oversampled and cannot be read as the overall mail mix. A random-cohort description requires the actual selection/embargo design, uncertainty, missingness and a clear disclaimer about model error. The 100 random development cases may have influenced the approach and are not held-out accuracy evidence; the 50 holdout cases are too few to validate rare-class accuracy.

The existing [60-row selector](../corpus-experiments/month-sample-select.py) is a reference for seeded ranking, private manifests and source rechecking. Its dates and quotas are hard-coded; do not run it unchanged for this pilot or Da Vinci.

## 3. Prepare evidence without losing the request

- Retain the current authored message, relevant earlier same-thread context available at that point, and source spans. Preserve quoted history when it carries the request; a reviewed reminder would otherwise lose its purpose. Do not carry facts from a different booking merely because it shares a thread. A stored timestamp alone does not prove a separate message was earlier: sender `Date` values can make a reply appear to precede its target. Require reliable received/ingest ordering and check message ancestry/`In-Reply-To`; exclude an actual reply to the target even if its stored time appears earlier. Where ordering or relationship cannot be established, withhold the separate context and mark the gap unknown. Quoted material already present in the target remains decision-time input, with authorship uncertainty. ## 5. Quality checks and reporting

- Use independently specified synthetic examples with expected labels to check 10 versus 11 rooms, multiple actions, an integration-failure notice, a quoted-only request, different stays in one thread, an inline receipt image, POP versus voucher, absent attachment evidence, and date/identifier preservation. Verify source-row sampling, fixed-seed replay, holdout isolation, private file permissions and no future-context leakage. Check both the helpers and the planned classifier against those examples; synthetic success does not establish real-mail accuracy or production behavior.
- Report cleanup loss candidates, cited-span failures, per-axis model agreement/disagreement, withheld/truncated/unknown rates, API/parse failures, and adjudicated semantic errors with their evidence and denominator. Record tokens/cost when available. Compare versions on the same frozen rows. A model echoing a source span or agreeing with another run is not independent proof of a correct label; later staff action supports an outcome claim only within its own coverage. Old action labels with different scope or future context cannot be repurposed as ground truth for this counterfactual.
- If holdout results require a material change, mark that run exploratory: once used to tune, the same holdout cannot establish final performance. Select a fresh check under a documented follow-on design. Do not claim a validated accuracy, a production pass, or a 95% confidence threshold from model agreement. No owner-approved performance bar exists for this pilot.
- For the later full run, define `E` as all saved inbound source rows, `K` as rows with at least one clearly actionable in-scope unit—including a document-only filing task with missing bytes—and `H` as rows with at least one in-scope unit labeled `complete` or `partial`. Report **H / E** for all inbound and **H / K** for clearly actionable, in-scope rows; report the rate as unavailable if its denominator is zero. These are row-level any-help rates, not the share of tasks helped. Count each row once, even when it has several actions; unresolved duplicate candidates remain visible, so rows are not claimed to be distinct delivered emails. Preserve groups, skipped-channel mail, unresolved eligibility and unknown help separately; do not silently treat unknowns as no work. Report task-level complete/partial counts and staff-required actions alongside the headline. The user indicated the in-scope measure should lead, with the all-inbound measure alongside it.
- For sensitivity to unresolved rows, let `U_h` be rows in `K` not already in `H` whose help is unknown, and `U_e` be rows outside `K` whose eligibility is unresolved and could be in scope. Alongside **H / E**, show `(H + U_h + U_e) / E`; alongside **H / K**, show the unknown-only range `H / (K + U_e)` to `(H + U_h + U_e) / (K + U_e)` when the denominator is nonzero. These conditional bounds address only the specified unknowns; they do not bound model mistakes, missing corpus coverage, linkage or scenario-mapping errors. Apply sampling uncertainty only when the design supports it.
- Treat `complete` and `partial` as hypothetical capability estimates, not observed use or fully automated resolutions. A file's presence, a document upload, a voucher and settled funds are different facts. Sender domain/no-reply alone cannot prove no work; failed-integration subjects are an explicit exception candidate. Record the current OTA exclusion versus source-code exception as `policy_conflict`, rather than inventing a routing decision. [Census and exception](centurion-origin-census.md#use-for-triage-research) [Current product rule](../../../context/PRODUCT-DETAIL.md#front-door-specifics)

The pilot is ready to inform a full run when the draw and source fingerprint replay, each usable row has two assessments or an explicit failure/abstention, evidence and missingness are recorded, development and holdout results are separate, and no known material cleanup loss is silently accepted. Summarize remaining disagreements and the attachment gap even if those checks pass. This is a readiness check for further research, not an accuracy guarantee.
