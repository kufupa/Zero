# Centurion corpus run

- Scope: 26 Sep 00:00–30 Oct 17:00, 2026, South Africa time; owner confirmed 26 Sep. Earlier mail is outside this run.
- Source: read-only Centurion snapshots `centurion-nine-day-bfcd9949-591f-4c9a-b061-3679ec793b51` and `centurion-nine-day-8774dd09-e874-46f8-bed7-a24e4c282711`; [frame comparison](october-frame-bridge.py) found 66 matching local/fresh day-direction groups, with 223 newer rows.
- Frame: 4,719 inbound and 2,099 outbound source rows. Merge rows with the same direction, Message-ID, normalized sender and subject: 4,516 inbound and 2,067 outbound. Source-row mappings are kept privately.
- Unit: actionable inbound reservation emails; exclude OTA/channel work. Count potential help once per inbound if any specified action is supported. Quotes, bookings, amendments, cancellations, POP/voucher filing and invoices are in scope. Flag >10 rooms for one group and keep them in the denominator; group-only work is out. A separate individual task can still count. Staff approval does not disqualify help. Action categories overlap.
- Replies: direct `In-Reply-To` first, then same thread or same recipient/time; Luna checks the action and guest/reference. Up to five candidate replies per inbound. A PMS confirmation template is a heuristic for system-generated mail, not proof of unattended sending.
- Preparation: [range builder](centurion-range-prepare.py), [recent tail](centurion-recent-tail.py), private `bulk-sep26-v7/`. Cleans quoted content and secrets; 136 current bodies exceed 4,000 chars, 1,352 quoted histories exceed 2,000. Another 103 inbound bodies contain a wrapped quote header the cleaner did not split. Original MIME exists for many emails; one inspected spreadsheet is summarized in private attachment observations.
- Bodyless messages: 215 inbound emails had neither current nor quoted body. 191 were guest-review notifications by sender and subject. For the other 24, [MIME recovery](centurion-recover-empty-mime.py) read original messages: 5 had recoverable body text and 17 had attachment names. The 17 with new evidence were reclassified; attachment contents were not read in this pass.
- Pilot: 50 random October rows; Haiku v5 and Luna medium agreed on 46/50 help decisions. Three disagreements concerned bank notices or hotel-sent copies; the fourth needed room count. A blinded Luna review agreed with Haiku on the inclusion/help decisions for 24 deliberately difficult examples. Agreement is not ground truth.
- Paired cleaning check: 16 changed inputs; one voucher result flipped when a prior request fell beyond 1,000 quoted characters. The final 2,000-character context retains that request. A later prompt inferred the voucher with either length, so this single example does not measure cleaning accuracy.
- Speed: 50 single-email Luna medium requests at concurrency 16 completed in 14.21 seconds; raw extrapolation for 6,583 emails is ~31 minutes. A four-case max-effort comparison was ~10 times slower at median. Bulk estimate: 35–60 minutes, subject to throttling/retries.
- Classifier: [Luna runner](luna-corpus-runner.py), [inbound prompt v12](2026-11-01-luna-opportunity-prompt-v12.md), [outbound prompt](2026-11-01-luna-outbound-prompt.md). Store:false; owner confirmed the configured Azure route and retention settings.
- Group review: v12 marked 21 group-only emails helpable. The [group addendum](2026-11-02-group-review-addendum.md) corrected those and passed five synthetic edge cases; an independent reviewer checked all 21. Two inclusion disputes and one missed group tag were resolved with reasons kept privately. Retain the group label regardless of the help decision.
- Reply audit: an independent 30-case stratified review found one extra linked reply for a different stay and one extra booking tag; both are recorded as corrections. No matching reply was found in the 10 sampled `none_linked` cases. This is a diagnostic sample, not a corpus-level precision estimate.
- Per-email triage: retain the requested actions, proposed Nava action, and evidence of any linked reply as separate fields. Treat this as an LLM-labelled candidate dataset, not independent ground truth.
- Blind holdout: independent Luna review agreed with bulk on inclusion and helpability for 29/30 fresh random emails. The other had a `POP` subject but its only PDF was an unpaid pro-forma, so bulk exclusion was retained. Agreement is not a human gold standard.
- Provider limit: one inbound was rejected by Azure's content filter after retry; category not supplied. It remains unclassified in the exported dataset. The other 4,515 inbound and all 2,067 outbound emails were classified.
- Remaining: finish action-level descriptions, check the voucher category and report estimated metrics. No independent human-labelled accuracy set exists.
