# Mailbox opportunity analysis

Goal: estimate what Nava **could** help with for a dated mailbox, then retain a reviewable label and proposed action for each email. This is not measured adoption.

1. **Frame.** Select property, mailbox and timezone-aware date range. Preserve source IDs and envelope sender, To and Cc. Deduplicate transport copies by direction, normalized Message-ID, sender and subject; retain the source-to-email mapping. Record both raw and deduplicated counts. To/Cc is context, not proof of direction or intent: distinguish a copied outgoing email from an internal instruction sent to the hotel.
2. **Clean.** Remove HTML, images, links and credentials; split repeated quoted text and retain a bounded earlier excerpt. Trim signatures only after a paired check that useful text is retained. Track truncation. Do not discard an active forwarded request merely because the forward has no new body.
3. **Inspect attachments.** Build a MIME attachment inventory for the entire inbound frame, distinguishing confirmed absence, unavailable data and unscanned cases. Keep filenames and types; remove inline brand images. Prioritize missing group-size and document decisions for bounded PDF/XLSX text extraction. Bind observations to the source-packet hash; record unreadable/scanned files and oversized excerpts instead of treating them as empty. Where invoice delivery matters, inventory linked outbound attachments too. A filename is evidence of a likely document type, not proof of its contents.
4. **Find possible replies.** Prefer direct reply headers, then same thread or recipient/time candidates. Include weekend-spanning windows. Also search backwards from sent messages: a small inbound-side candidate limit can hide HMS confirmations. For model review, group messages only when subject, recipients and complete available bodies match; retain every original message in the counts and the group mapping. Require the model to match the request and stay; same guest or close timing alone is insufficient. Record linked source IDs and keep unanswered inbound emails in the frame.
5. **Classify each inbound with an LLM.** Separate source/OTA notices, actionable reservation work, workflow labels, group scope, prospective Nava help and actual visible replies. Quotes, creates, amendments, cancellations, reservation invoices and filing supplied proofs/vouchers are hypothetical supported tasks; a >10-room group is tracked and group-only work is excluded. Human approval does not remove the opportunity. Use quoted context to understand an active follow-up, but do not revive completed or unrelated work. Distinguish staff instructions from copies of hotel-authored outgoing mail; do not count venue-only event enquiries as stay quotes.
6. **Review consistency.** Recheck group work marked helpable, help without a supported action, an agency voucher excluded as OTA, and mismatched replies or invoices. Retrieve other messages with similar subjects when they may reveal the same stay's size, then check party, dates and actual room block: subject similarity alone does not establish group scope. Search the complete available property history before slicing periods; the Centurion check here ran within each period. Count overlapping rooms in a single block even with staggered arrivals; do not sum separate stays or room-nights. An event's attendance is not automatically its accommodation count. Releasing unsold inventory is not an amendment to a guest booking. An active forward may assign work; a hotel-written copy may not. Re-review missed replies separately, match both task and stay, and preserve reasoned corrections alongside original results.
7. **Create action-level rows.** Describe the request, what Nava could have done when the email arrived, and what later replies actually say. A later completion does not erase the original opportunity or authorize doing it again now. The owner states that a Centurion staff acknowledgement of receiving a supplied payment proof means it was filed; record that inferred outcome separately from the literal reply and from payment settlement. Existing saved rows predate this rule. Current detail passes see later replies, so their `state` can reflect those replies; the dataset flags this and is not a leak-free triage benchmark. Link future training/evaluation inputs to the message as it was at arrival and keep later outcomes separate. Include input/prompt/model hashes, source mappings and missing-evidence flags. These are candidate labels, not independent ground truth.
8. **Measure quality.** For future edge-case tests, use real emails as the owner requested; the synthetic tests in the historical run record remain historical evidence. Evaluate a fresh, reproducible sample that includes ordinary emails and rare boundaries. Compare independent reviews, investigate disagreements, and record coverage, errors, uncertainty and provider blocks. Model agreement is not proof of accuracy. Track attachment availability and response-link limitations.
9. **Report.** Show total inbound/outbound, actionable reservation emails, potential-help count and percentage, group emails, overlapping workflow counts, staff/PMS response estimates and main sender domains. State the exact window and denominator. For responses, separately count distinct linked outbound messages and inbound requests with a link: one response may cover several inbound messages and one inbound may have several replies. A staff reply linked to a helpable request does not mean Nava would itself send that reply.

Centurion-specific execution details and current results: [run record](2026-11-02-centurion-corpus-run.md). Before repeating for DaVinci, supply its own mailbox transport, snapshots, date range and source mappings; never reuse Centurion counts or assume schemas match.

## Reusable tools

- [Snapshot export](mailbox-snapshot-export.py): named, pre-saved snapshots + timezone-aware `--start`/`--end` → cleaned private source rows. Supply the correct property snapshots; the script does not infer ownership.
- [Frame builder](mailbox-frame-build.py): normalized `--input` + mailbox/date range → private `inbound.jsonl`, `outbound.jsonl`, `manifest.jsonl`. Repeated source IDs with differing rows stop the run unless ordered snapshot precedence is explicitly chosen. On Centurion it reproduced all 6,583 packets except one separately added document observation.
- [Reply candidate supplement](mailbox-reply-candidate-supplement.py) and [frame merge](mailbox-merge-candidate-supplement.py): add candidates found from the sent side, verify the original packets and outbound mappings, and retain a private mapping of identical messages. The reverse search itself is capped at three inbound candidates per outbound, so this improves recall without proving it is complete.
- [Unlinked reply selection](mailbox-unlinked-reply-review-select-v2.py) and [reply-only overlay](mailbox-reply-review-overlay.py): recheck missed candidates and preserve an audit. Independently reject mismatched reply edges by case and source-bound outbound ID; test the baseline as well as recovered links. An unlinked email may still have received a response.
- [MIME index](mailbox-mime-index.py) and [joiner](mailbox-augment-attachments.py): pass the frame's inbound packets and property snapshot names; inventories are private and body recovery runs only for blank cleaned messages. [Merge shards](mailbox-mime-merge-shards.py) checks source hashes and coverage before combining parallel runs. [Indexer instructions](mailbox-mime-index.md). Use [document evidence extraction](centurion-attachment-evidence.py) for selected PDFs/spreadsheets whose contents determine a material decision; it emits evidence, not labels.
- [Attachment selection](centurion-attachment-review-helper.py), [available-document selection](mailbox-available-attachment-selection.py), [bounded extraction](mailbox-attachment-batch.py), and [casewise merge](mailbox-attachment-evidence-merge-batch.py) select and extract documents with source hashes and coverage statuses. Selectors do not assign labels.
- [Legacy XLS evidence](centurion-xls-evidence.py) checks old spreadsheets with a pinned, temporary parser and records source/attachment hashes and scan limits. A bounded scan with no room-count cue does not prove the workbook contains no relevant information.
- [Semantic review selection](mailbox-semantic-review-select.py) routes selected workflows, all reservation work, uncertain cases or high-attendance candidates to a model; selection does not assign group labels. The [envelope index](mailbox-augment-inbound-envelope.py) rebuilds source-bound To/Cc. The [outbound audience report](mailbox-outbound-audience-report.py) separates observed external recipients from internally addressed messages; missing Cc is not proof of no external recipient.
- [Related group-context selection](mailbox-group-context-review-select.py) retrieves subject-related, already group-tagged messages and records source hashes. It creates candidates, not group labels. Have an LLM compare the actual stay and task; audit changes and retain the original result. Its subject-prefix rule will miss other relevant conversations.
- [LLM runner](luna-corpus-runner.py): one email per call, no tools, `store:false`, input/prompt/model hashes, resumable outputs. Pass the current prompt explicitly. Carry forward a provider-blocked case with `--exclude-case`; do not resubmit it or drop it from the frame.
- [Compose reviewed layers](mailbox-compose-reviewed.py): validate packet and prompt identity before overlaying semantic re-reviews; preserve the earlier results and a change audit. Use the run record for the exact ordered prompts.
- [Blind sample](mailbox-sample-review.py): reproducible 150 random + 100 targeted cases; reviewer sees packets, not prior labels. Use `--exclude-selection` to keep a fresh sample separate from examples already used to revise the prompt. [Freeze aggregate labels](mailbox-freeze-comparison.py) binds the final labels to those exact packets and marks them as derived, not a new model invocation; [compare](compare-blind-review.py) reports the two groups separately. [Synthetic comparison](luna-v14-synthetic-regression-check.py) checks expected fixture fields.
- [Aggregate](centurion-aggregate-opportunity.py) keeps the denominator, overlapping workflow counts, group count, replies and domains distinct. [Action details](luna-action-details.py) separates requests, hypothetical Nava steps and observed replies; v6/v7 make explicit inferences for omitted room counts and unavailable attachments. After a correction, [rerun selection](mailbox-action-rerun-selection.py) chooses changed packets and output-validation failures; [changed selection](mailbox-action-changed-selection.py) compares a previously joined dataset with new inputs. Both preserve provider or sensitive-data holds. [Dataset join](mailbox-dataset-join.py) accepts explicitly named prompt versions, reuses results only when projected inputs match, and flags no-step, group-context, both group/action-scope mismatch directions and temporal-state disagreements. The [Centurion dataset wrapper](centurion-20261102-datasets.sh) reproduces this run's two outputs from their saved layers.
