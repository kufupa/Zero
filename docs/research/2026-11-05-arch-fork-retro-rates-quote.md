## Recommendation reachability and numeric override split

The code proves the recommendation gap more narrowly than “backend missing”:

- rate-options.ts has an advisory recommendRateCandidate path: explicit typed rate first, unchanged Amend rate second; absent those, Create/HRCEN uses verified bound-party history, then email frequency and full-source fallback. It never silently replaces a staff rate.
- pane-rate-recommendation.ts uses a dated private frequency snapshot for email/full history. Bound-party history depends on prior prepared staff rate picks matched by exact current native IDs; historical party labels are not joined by name. Thus a cold new party may have no party-specific history even if email/full prior exists.
- ContextualRateCheck returns early when rate is empty and starts rateCandidates with typedRatePlan: rate. Amend’s useAmendRateCandidates also returns early when its typed/current rate is empty. With a code, candidate options are filtered to that selected code and the backend emits explicit_request/unchanged_hms, not an alternative frequency recommendation. The returned recommendation text is rendered, but it cannot surface a useful no-code suggestion. Backend capability alone therefore does not satisfy R5/P26–P28.
- The final plan C5/W5 covers selected-rate validation, not frequency suggestions. The later checkpoint’s explicit deferral list names numeric overrides/Retain but not frequency recommendation. No evidence of explicit owner cancellation; gap is omitted/unverified.

For amounts, [Quote rate-controls.tsx](/home/ubuntu/projects/zero/.worktrees/core-question-list-final-deploy-20261105/apps/mail/components/quote-workflow/rate-controls.tsx:147) requires a valid rate code, per-night amount and staff-only authorisation reason; its “Type a rate” and “Discount / override” actions feed a Quote row. It is not passed to Cloud Create/Amend as an HMS price setter, as Nov 4 rate research documents. Separately, the original U7 plan defines saved-stay OSSTWZ amount write, explicit Go and fresh nightly readback; Nov 5 C10/checkpoint defers that native numeric override/Retain branch. Do not merge these into one “override deferred” result.

The first output claim should remain exactly bounded: selected native offer → PDF/Excel contents and unsent cover were reported passed; actual Outlook/webmail insertion was not.
