## Saved-stay native sequence actually observed

1. Start from a **clean, exact saved HRCEN stay**. Click the visible `Stay Wizard` action. The captured open request was `POST /web/base/OSSTWZ`, with `SYSTEM_FUNCTION_NAME=OSSTWZ`, `USER_FUNCTION_NAME=OSSTWZ`, property, saved-stay ID, guest ID and confirmation. These identify the target; do not bind the entire request body. [Captured open](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/report-source59.json), [controller](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/native-save-source47.json).
2. The modal exposed Start Date, End Date, Availability Instruction, Rate Instruction, Rate, Rate Reason, the dated grid, and Submit. Initially `rateinstr=REQUOT` and amount/reason were read-only. Choosing **Override** sent `POST /web/base/OSSTWZ.RateInstructionChange` with `rateinstr=OVER`; its successful response made `rateamount` and `ratereason` editable. [Capture](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/report-source62.json).
3. The prior fake test set `startdate=enddate=20-Jan-2027`, `availabilityinstr=AVAIL` (**Available Only**), `rateamount=2620`, `ratecurr=ZAR`, and selected **Correct Rate**, native `ratereason=COR`. Room, room type and rate-plan change fields were blank in this case. The visible form marked dates, availability instruction, rate instruction, rate and reason required. `COR` is an observed option, not a universal default. [Runner](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/fixture64.mjs:89), [screenshot](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/wizard-one-night-override-fixture64.png).
4. **Submit is a write**, not a preview: `POST /web/base/OSSTWZ.UpdateStayWizard` included the target, date range, instruction, amount, currency and reason. It also had `ONLY_DATA_REQUIRED=true` and refresh flags; those flags did not make it read-only. In that previous run Submit changed the saved record. Fresh Rate Details readback returned three nights **R2,610 / R2,610 / R2,620**, with the other checked stay details preserved. Projected subtotal and total were R7,840, with R0 tax and R0 fees for that fixture. [Prior result](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/report-fixture64.json), [fresh rates](/tmp/nava-u3-rate-probe-HLLwep/fresh-fixture64.json).

The same-date start/end proved a **single inclusive night**. Infor describes a date range for the change and one Rate field, but a uniform multi-night HRCEN override and multiple different requested amounts in one operation are unproven. [Infor Stay Wizard guide](https://docs.infor.com/hms/2026.09/en-us/hmsolh/s_t1424428573998.html). Do not infer atomic multi-night application from the one-night result.

Infor documents **Allow Rate Overrides** on a rate plan and `AUTHRTOVRD` manager authorization as possible gates; mobile supervisor approval can also appear on Stay Wizard or Guest Stay. Their effective values for HRCEN, other rates and users are unknown. One account completed the captured fake case; do not assume every account or plan can override or auto-approve. [Rate-plan guide](https://docs.infor.com/hms/3.8/en-us/hmsolh/s_t1424428595902.html), [field authorization](https://docs.infor.com/hms/3.8/en-us/hmsitolh/s_t1424428539270.html), [supervisor approval](https://docs.infor.com/hms/2026.08/en-us/hmsolh/xff1620369741446.html).
