## Goal Capsule

Deliver voucher and payment-proof filing, saved-stay date/occupancy/amount amendment, and multi-room booking. Each must be discoverable and usable in the shared staff pane in Outlook and webmail, connected to the real server and HMS Cloud path, and honestly report the final result. Use general, property-aware production tools protected by enforceable authorization, then verify with representative Labtest records. Keep Nava replies unsent; HMS confirmation email for real-guest create follows the owner's separate pilot decision, never for Labtest.

**Why now:** the retrospective [case-mix sample](../research/halloween/b2b-dashboard/README.md) reports 1,361 actionable emails, with 372 voucher, 90 payment-proof and 118 amendment labels. Labels are model-generated, overlap, and are not verified filing success or time savings. Multi-room demand needs its own measurement. These counts guide priority, not an exclusion rule.

**Current evidence:** attachment identity/approval exists, but the Cloud uploader is Labtest-only and current document readback is metadata-only. Date and adult stages exist but are not proven saved; HMS repricing prompted an unresolved rate-retention dialog. Quote overrides are not evidence of saved-stay amount editing. Room choices exist in the pane but a durable batch create does not. See `apps/server-pilot/tooling/hms-cloud/{document-attachment,guest-stay-amend-adults,guest-stay-amend-date-room-rate}.md`, `apps/mail/components/quote-workflow/{voucher-find-card,amend-lookup-panel,booking-card}.tsx`, and the connected-pilot plan.

## Product Contract

### Voucher and proof of payment

1. Staff open the original email, see available eligible attachments and the selected stay, choose the exact document and confirm the destination. Extracted surname, arrival, amount and document type are suggestions, not a unique identity or proof of payment. Conflicting or multiple stays require selection; no silent match.
2. Bind mailbox/property, message, attachment, mandatory SHA-256 and confirmed stay identity to the DB approval. Validate source bytes and MIME rather than trusting a filename; current executor accepts PDF only. Discover HMS-supported document types, including voucher and proof of payment, and preserve the staff-recognizable filename/type. Support additional formats HMS can actually accept; where not yet supported, say why the item cannot be filed, and record the coverage gap rather than calling all filing complete. Do not silently convert attachments.
3. Staff approve once for the exact operation. Create a random non-secret upload-operation marker, not the bearer grant UUID, and place it in an HMS-supported name or description along with the source name. Prove that the marker survives HMS truncation/normalization and uniquely identifies a document. **Before the upload can leave the browser**, durably append the marker, pre-upload document list and stable IDs to the run record or `work_event`. Reopen the same stay in a fresh session and identify the newly persisted document unambiguously against that preimage and marker. An indistinguishable existing document or partial folder view is uncertain, not success. Metadata plus screenshot proves only visible metadata; claim matching stored bytes only when a supported download/hash method proves it. Show **filed**, **failed**, or **outcome needs checking**, never success on dispatch alone. Preserve evidence to reconcile an uncertain attempt before another upload.
4. Mark proof as a document filed. A claimed payment amount is source evidence or staff input, not a posted or cleared payment. No financial ledger write is implied.

### Saved-stay amendment

1. Staff select the exact existing stay, inspect its current dates, occupancy and amount, then choose requested changes. Retain original email context. The UI must expose date, adult and applicable child occupancy, and amount, rather than presenting a dead control or text-only promise. Show unspecified child-age or rate details as unknown and request the information HMS actually requires.
2. Show current vs requested values and distinguish requested, estimated and HMS-read-back price. Discover which native amount overrides, rate-plan changes and discount semantics HMS actually provides. Staff select the applicable supported basis; make currency, per-night/per-room versus stay total, taxes and authorization explicit. Do not substitute a rate-plan change for an amount override or invent a total. Require “Authorised by” for saved-stay discounts as a conservative extension of the existing quote rule; whether it confers real-guest pricing authority remains a separate owner hold.
3. On fresh Labtest stays, discover the saved amount control and map the native rate-retention prompt's actual choices and resulting rate/amount behavior. A native dialog after a possible Save is uncertain under the existing adapter: reconcile that record before further actions and never blindly repeat it. Use the fewest independent fake stays needed to distinguish effects. If pricing basis or prompt behavior remains unknown, do not guess; hold that save while other independent work continues. Never accept a cancellation-penalty dialog.
4. Implement dialog handling as a new, tested Cloud adapter capability, not a switch on the current uncertain-only adapter. Check fresh stay identity, inventory, pricing and preimage. Present actual price/rate consequences and the chosen supported response before staff commit. Bind approved changes and reviewed preimage to the durable DB single-use grant and the `hms_write` executor; retire or fence the process-local preview grant for this new path. Save, capture a screenshot, reopen/read back, and show exact confirmed values and discrepancies. A stale preimage requires another review; any uncertain save requires reconciliation before retry.

### Multi-room booking

1. Staff see the requested room types and counts in the shared booking flow, with dates, occupancy, guest/party, rate and applicable amount. Follow the V1 pane rule: no generic “Room 1 / Room 2” labels. Distinguish individual child stays by meaningful guest, room-type and confirmation details where needed. Changes to any room invalidate the approved manifest. Show estimates as estimates and missing mandatory fields before approval. Do not silently merge distinct parties or assume identical rooms.
2. Resolve a separate batch capability, independent of single-create mode. Initially it permits only `lab`, with the strict Cloud Proof Labtest identity check on **every** child; reject any real-guest multi-room manifest before claim or dispatch even when single-create mode is `on`. Preserve the separately authorized single-room path. Approval covers an immutable ordered manifest bound to mailbox, property, source, party, booking values and pricing. Use one `hms-write:<grant UUID>` run and consume its single-use grant once for the batch. Implement durable child claims and outcomes outside the approved form, using the existing append-only `work_event` log. Under a row lock, atomically check for an earlier child claim and append the new claim; release the lock before HMS. A crash after claim is uncertain even if dispatch might not have occurred. Extend the lost-lease reconciliation path rather than auto-requeueing.
3. Acquire the property lock separately for each child; inside it take a fresh, single-use preimage and recheck grant expiry, manifest digest and authority before dispatch and immediately before Save. Existing Labtest preimages expire after five minutes; existing approval expires with its snapshot, at most 30 minutes from the snapshot read. Measure child duration and account for remaining grant lifetime when showing a conservative batch limit before approval. If authority expires, stop and obtain fresh approval for remaining rooms. Define a create-specific before-state digest: allow only independently confirmed siblings from this authorized batch and halt on ambiguous matches or unrelated changes. Do not blanket-bypass duplicate checks. Default to serial child dispatch until safe concurrency is demonstrated.
4. Each room gets a distinct confirmed reservation and independent screenshot/readback. On partial failure or uncertainty, show each child's state and identifiers; reconcile before retry and never repeat confirmed or uncertain children. Show unattempted rooms as unattempted, not failed. A restart cannot automatically repeat a claimed child.
5. Native association and batch atomicity are not assumed. Prior HRDAV evidence reports a legacy linked path ([active handoff](../context/ACTIVE.md#hrdav-full-field-booking-run--2026-10-28-opus-coordinator-asks-362364)), but that does not prove Cloud/HRCEN linking. The new batch path must reject or bypass `parentConfirmation` and never spawn the legacy `add-room-stay.mjs` linker; test this without breaking unrelated existing routes. Do not repeat previously denied linker probes. Describe independent rooms honestly unless a separately authorized and proven native association is available; do not label independent rooms linked or one atomic reservation.

### Parallelism and scheduling

After the pilot and review gate, start the filing, amendment and batch lanes alongside the shared write-runner owner. Each lane owns its UI and mount. First run focused discovery in parallel with safe contract/component coding: amend maps native pricing and the rate dialog; filing maps document codes, supported formats and reconciliation; batch measures child timing and fresh preimages. These are discovery gates, not permission to dispatch uncertain operations. On a native unknown, stop that action, report it and keep independent lanes moving. Do not spend the entire target window gathering evidence before building.

The shared executor is a true prerequisite for all three complete product paths. Actual amend save depends on native pricing and dialog semantics. Filing success depends on a uniquely verifiable persisted document. Batch execution depends on the pilot's single-stay Cloud writer, property locks, grants and sibling-aware preimages. UI/contract work may proceed in parallel, but mocks do not satisfy any of those dependencies. Current `scripts/orch/dispatch.sh` gates dependencies on merge and locks conflicting file names; do not assume old dispatcher behavior or bypass it.

Use verified property-level locks and distinct fake records, not an unsupported universal one-browser restriction. Remote tests and typechecks share the approved lock: queue them while coding and review continue. The deployment worktree is shared and dirty; inspect and preserve it, use only the approved deploy path, and stop if safe deployment is blocked.

The write runner and amount discovery are separate critical-path items with unmeasured durations; neither is safely priced at zero. Record elapsed time and re-estimate after those checks and the post-pilot baseline are available, using the ETA ledger before quoting a numeric forecast.
