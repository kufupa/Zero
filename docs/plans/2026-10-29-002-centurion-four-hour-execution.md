# Centurion overnight execution

The owner wants as many complete workflows as possible, aiming for four hours and continuing through seven hours or longer if needed. This is an aim, not a deadline or evidence that the whole product will be ready. This plan narrows the existing [first wave](2026-10-29-001-feat-centurion-overnight-first-wave-plan.md) to Centurion and supersedes its timing estimates. Reuse the existing Outlook/webmail pane, jobs and HMS tools; add or replace files when that is the shortest safe path.

## Order and evidence

1. **Quote first.** Get a selected Centurion email through the existing extraction and staff quote job, a live SaaS availability read with actual eligible company/agency context when applicable, verified nightly prices and total, a priced PDF, and an editable unsent reply. Start with an explicitly adult-only case, then support missing details and children without converting an unknown age or count into zero. The HMS-native provisional-quote meaning remains a separate discovery question; a PDF alone does not prove it.
2. **One-room Labtest create.** Connect the reviewed server-owned plan to the real pane and existing job. Before Save, verify the property, case, guest, rate and current approved nightly prices, hold the relevant monitored property locks, and suppress any unapproved confirmation. Afterward, independently read back the booking and commercial fields. Reconcile an uncertain result while the job is alive; never blindly retry or create a dependent room while the parent is unknown. Report partial or uncertain outcomes accurately.
3. **Expand the create workflow.** Add multiple distinct rooms/guests, native Add-Ons and Guest Details once the SaaS screens and charge/confirmation sequencing are observed. Keep one staff approval for the displayed plan and preserve each step's result. Do not claim one complete HMS confirmation until it is observed; actual sending requires applicable authority.
4. **Amend, then lab-only cancel, then payment.** Reuse the existing runners and the first booking where its lifecycle permits. Verify actual SaaS behavior and independent readback. A document or voucher is not a posted payment; refusing a penalty prompt does not prove an earlier cancellation did not occur.

The first quote and booking are smoke tests, not staff-readiness evidence. Treat a failed or denied live probe as a specific blocker: diagnose without bypassing it, and continue independent permitted work. Follow the active-workflow order in `PRODUCT.md`.

After the first hour, prioritize whichever dependency most directly unlocks the quote; around four hours, reassess from measured proof and continue the remaining authorized scope. Do not manufacture a feature count or release ETA.
