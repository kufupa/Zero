**Map:** [Centurion end-to-end reservation agent](../map.md)

## Question

How can the current Outlook/webmail pane show all relevant HMS fields, party and rate choices, multiple rooms, Add-Ons, confirmation recipient and action outcomes clearly enough for one staff approval?

Use existing V1 layout and owner feedback as constraints. Prototype with synthetic cases, including missing information, changed rates, a partial success and an uncertain result; preserve staff edits as background prefill arrives. Compare compact alternatives only to answer the review question. The prototype is throwaway and is not production implementation or permission to send.
