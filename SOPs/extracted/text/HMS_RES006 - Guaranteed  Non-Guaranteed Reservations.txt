Responsibilities:  
  
Reservations  
 
Policy: 
1. Each guest, travel agent or tour operator making a booking should be advised that their booking is 
not guaranteed unless one of the following is provided: 
 
1.1 Travel agent voucher / voucher number (Voucher to be inserted into “Documents” on the 
reservation if the property uses INFOR HMS) if the Travel Agency has credit facilities with 
the hotel. 
 
1.2 100% Deposit pre-paid into our bank account on FIT (Individual) bookings at least 72 hours 
prior to the guest’s arrival (if a group then group contract applies) 
 
1.3 Payment has been made in full and confirmed with the Accounts department that the 
payment reflects in the bank. 
 
1.4 Order number sent to the Hotel should the company have an account with the hotel. 
 
1.5 Payment has been completed via the Legacy Online payment portal, monies have been 
received into IVERI and reflect on the Hotels bank statement. 
 
Procedure:  
1. Cancellation Policy 
 
If a booking has been guaranteed the guest must be advised of the cancellation policy.  
 
1.1 Cancellations - for two or less rooms – within 48 hours prior to arrival will be subject to a full 
cancellation fee of the value of the accommodation booked.   
 
1.2 Cancellations - for three to nine rooms – within seven days prior to arrival will be subject to a full 
cancellation fee of the value of the accommodation booked.  
 
1.3 Cancellations – 10 rooms or more, the group cancellation policy will apply.

If a booking has not been guaranteed the guest must be advised that the hotel reserves the right to cancel the 
booking at 6pm on the day of arrival or if otherwise stated on the terms and conditions on the confirmation 
letter. 
 
The hotel also reserves the right to make alternative accommodation arrangements for non -guaranteed 
bookings if the hotel is fully booked. 
 
Deviations: 
 
Please note that deviations to the above policy & procedure are permitted if the outcome of the above does not 
have a negative impact on the business of the Hotel or Legacy Hotels & Resorts.  All deviations must be authorized 
by the Supervisor or Manager of the Department and must be documented for auditing purposes. The Supervisor 
and/or Manager must use their discretion on the deviation if it impacts negatively the relationship and future 
business from the client, as long as the decision of the deviation is justifiable
