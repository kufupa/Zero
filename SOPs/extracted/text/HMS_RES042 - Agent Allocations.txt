Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations, Reservations Manager, Rooms Division Manager & General Manager 
 
Policy: 
 
Room allocations are given to agents/consortia by Legacy Hotels & Resorts by the sales & marketing 
department.  The room allocation amount is approved by the Group Sales Manager and the General 
Manager of the property. Allocations are given to agents to make the booking process easier for the 
agent. 
  
Room allocations must be managed by the reservations department. Optimised yield of the allocation 
stock needs to be monitored and managed with regards to release dates, allocations taken, and 
available allocations not used. This must be adhered to maximise on occupancies of the hotel. 
 
Purpose:   
 
Scope:  
 
Definitions:   
 
Procedure:  
 
1. Agent/Company Allocations 
The Reservations Manager and/or Rooms Division Manager must draw up a list of the Agents/Companies 
that have Allocations with the hotel. This list must have the agent’s name, number of rooms on allocations 
and the release period.  The list must be given to the reservations department and all persons dealing with 
allocations to be told and made aware of this list. 
 
Allocations must  be inserted into the  INFOR HMS with the correct cut off days/Dates and close out 
during close out specified dates.
