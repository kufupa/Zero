Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations  
 
Policy: 
 
The reservationist needs to be able to answer general reservation enquiries. 
 
Procedure:  
 
1. General Reservation Enquiries 
 
The reservationist must have full knowledge of the following information: 
 
1.1 Lay out of hotel. 
 
1.2 Amount of rooms 
 
1.3 Room types and amounts of each room. 
 
1.4 General information on rooms, layout, specifications, etc. 
 
1.5 Rates and conditions of each rate category 
 
1.6 General facilities of the Hotel. 
 
1.7 Operation times of departments and set prices of meals. 
 
1.8 Transport costs to airport and nearby attractions. 
 
1.9 Nearby activities and attractions for guests. 
 
1.10 General knowledge of other Legacy Properties
