Document ID Title 
HMS_RES024 CANCELLATION POLICY 
Revision:  2 Prepared By: Systems Development Date Revised: 03/05/2024 
Issue Date:   Approved By: Andrew Wytrykowski Date Approved:  
 
Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations and General Manager 
 
Policy: 
 
When the client cancels the reservation within the cancellation policy, the client must be advised of 
the cancellation charge. Room revenue must be raised in INFOR HMS for the cancellation charge and 
a copy of the invoice and letter sent to the client. 
 
Should the reservations department deem that the cancellation charge should be waivered; this should 
be given to the General Manager for approval. 
 
Purpose:   
 
Scope:  
 
Definitions:   
 
Procedure:  
 
 
Deviations: 
 
Please note that deviations to the above policy & procedure are permitted if the outcome of the above does not 
have a negative impact on the business of the Hotel or Legacy Hotels & Resorts.  All deviations must be authorized 
by the Supervisor or Manager of the Department and must be documented f or auditing purposes. The Supervisor 
and/or Manager must use their discretion on the deviation if it impacts negatively the relationship and future 
business from the client, as long as the decision of the deviation is justifiable.
