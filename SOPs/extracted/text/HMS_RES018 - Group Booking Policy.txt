Responsibilities:   
 
Group Reservationist and/or Banqueting reservations 
 
Policy: 
 
The Group Reservationist and/or Banqueting reservations will handle all Group bookings. 
 
The standard Legacy Hotels & Resorts Leisure/ G&C or Wholesale contracts will apply to all group 
reservations.  
 
Purpose:  
  
Scope:  
 
Definitions:  
  
G & C – Group and Convention 
FIT – Foreign Independent Traveler 
Wholesale – STO (Standard Tour Operator) 
 
Procedure:  
 
1. Group Reservations 
 
The Group Reservationist is responsible for the following: 
 
1.1. Should the group booking be made telephonically, take down all relevant details including name of 
company, dates, scope of booking etc.  Make the reservation in the property management system and 
after the booking is made, ask for correspondence by either fax or e-mail to confirm details of group 
reservation. 
 
1.2. The Group Booking turnaround times must be adhered to as follows: 
 
1.2.1. Any enquiry with regards to availability, rates, banqueting facilities and any other requested 
information from a Client must be answered within 3 hours.

1.2.2. If a group booking is to be made - Confirmation of group booking, terms and conditions and 
payment agreements to be sent to Client within 12 hours. 
 
1.3. Obtaining the reservation requests from the company/operator on a letter head or registered company e-
mail 
 
 
1.4. Issuing the correct contract to the company/operator by fax or e-mail, the following contracts will 
apply: 
 
1.4.1. Complete the standard Legacy Leisure/G&C contract according to the time period that the group 
is booked. 
 
1.4.2. Complete the standard Legacy Wholesale group contract according to the time period the group 
is booked if the booking is not a bona fide tour group. 
 
1.4.3. The Contract must be checked and signed by the head of department before being sent out 
to the client. 
 
1.4.4. Wholesale terms and conditions can only be applied to bona fide inbound leisure groups or FIT's. 
Any groups staying for the purpose of attending conferences, special town events, association 
meetings or any purpose other than leisure travel will be subject to Legacy's Group and 
Conference contract. 
 
NB! Other relevant info for certain groups may be added to the contract, but no 
information may be omitted or deleted from the original contract. 
 
1.4.5. Ensure that the signed copy of the terms and conditions are returned to the hotel on the date 
specified in the group contract 
 
1.4.6. Ensure that the Terms and Conditions are adhered to with regards to rooming lists, deposit due 
dates, confirmation dates and billing instructions 
 
1.4.7. Deposit request fields in Opera to be completed as per the contract sent out to the client, and 
follow ups to be done by printing the deposit request report on a daily basis to ensure deposit due 
dates are adhered to by the client. Be sure to specify whether the deposit is for rooms or catering 
when inserting the request and deposit received. 
 
1.4.8. Completing the reservation accurately and all profile details are entered and correct.  
 
2. The following rate codes will apply to non bona fide groups: 
 
2.1. Wholesale business will remain on the original code Wholesale contracted according to meal plans of 
each hotel. 
 
2.2. Non Bona Fide STO business will be made under Negotiated Contracts according to meal plans of each 
hotel.
