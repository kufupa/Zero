Wholesale (“STO”) TERMS AND CONDITIONS 
 
1. This contract is entered into between Legacy Hotels & Resorts (Pty) Limited (‘‘Legacy Hotels & 
Resorts”) and the under signed Tour Operator (“Tour Operator”) and shall come into force upon 
signature by both of the parties hereto. 
2. The Laws of the Republic of South Africa shall in all respects, govern this contract, except if a dispute 
is entered into in one of the other African countries where Legacy Hotels & Resorts operates, where 
the relevant laws in that country will apply. 
3. The applicable Tour Operator rates (“contract rates”) are as set out in this contract.  
a. All preferred rates are subject to availability and may not apply during special town events/peak 
periods. Best Available Rates will apply during these periods. 
b. The Namibian Dollar is deemed for the purposes of this agreement to be equivalent to the South 
African Rand. 
c. Rates quoted in Namibian Dollars are inclusive of VAT @ 15%.  
d. Rates quoted in South African Rands are inclusive of VAT @ 1 5%.   
e. For Gabon: Rates quoted in EUR are inclusive of VAT @ 18% and City Tax @ 2% (CFA 1.000/2 EUR 
per night per room).   
4. All the quoted rates include Tourism Levy which is in place or may be imposed in the future. Legacy 
Hotels & Resorts reserves the right to amend the quoted rates should there be any change in the 
percentage rate of VAT, or any other levy or tax, or unforeseen and unbudgeted for increases in labour 
and key commodities, or impost becoming payable by Legacy Hotels & Resorts after the date of 
printing of this contract or in the event of the devaluation of the SA Rand, Namibian Dollar against the 
US Dollar, GBP and Euro.

5. All rates in this ag reement quoted are on a per room per day basis (unless otherwise indicated) and 
are nett and non-commissionable.  
6. Legacy Hotels & Resorts reserves the right at its discretion to suspend the operation of this agreement 
during major events, conferences, or sporting events that on account of the extraordinary demand for 
accommodation during that period, Legacy Hotels & Resorts may not be in a position to offer the tour 
operator accommodation at the rates and on the terms and conditions set out in this agreement. Best 
available rates will apply during these events. 
7. Any rates which include a component of third party charges e.g. National Park entrance fees may be 
changed without notice should this component fee be increased.  
8. The quoted rates include accommodation and meals as specified. 
9. The rate for day use will be the single room rate, but the room must be vacated prior to 17h00, and is 
subject to availability. 
 
10. Wholesale Leader Discount:  
Accommodation/Breakfast 
(Based on tour leader in single accommodation). 
Group of 15 paying pax - one tour leader 
Group of 30 paying pax - two tour leaders 
For 31+ pax you will need to negotiate with Hotel Management if you require more tour leader rooms.  
Where Tour Leader Discounts are granted, lunch and dinner rates will apply (if partaken).  
Please note Tshukudu and Kuzuko tour leader policy is at Management’s discretion.  
 
11. The hotel requires a final rooming list 30 days prior to any group’s arrival.  
   Check-in time:  16h00 
Check-out time:   10h00 
Hotels will endeavor to accommodate groups for an earlier check-in, if required, but this is dependent 
on availability of rooms. Agents must liaise directly with the hotel concerned.  
12. Extended Payment facilities must be applied for with each individual hotel. 
13. These conditions will apply to all existing and newly established Legacy Hotels & Resorts properties 
during the specific validity period. 
14. Wholesale Terms and Conditions  can only be applied to bona fide inbound leisure groups or FIT's, 
however the property reserves the right to provide a specific group contract/agreement in addition to 
this contract.  Any groups staying for the purpose of attending con ferences, special town events, 
association meetings or any  purpose other than leisure travel will be subject to Legacy's Group and 
Conference terms and conditions, which are available on request.  
 
 
TOURS 
1. TOUR CANCELLATIONS/AMENDMENTS 
1.1. All tour cancellations/amendments must be given in writing to the relevant hotel.  
1.2. Cancellations/Amendments are subject to the following conditions: 
1.2.1. More than 60 days prior to the tour’s arrival at the hotel, the agent has the right to cancel or 
amend the tour, by committing themselves to a different number of rooms required.  
1.2.2. 45 to 60 days prior to the tour’s arrival at the hotel, the agent has the right to cancel 10% of the 
rooms they are holding on that date, without any cancellation fee payable. If, however, more 
than 10% of rooms originally booked are cancelled, the hotel reserves the right to levy a charge

2. FIT CANCELLATION POLICY  
2.1. Rooms will be kept until 18h00 on day of arrival unless otherwise assured or guaranteed.  
2.2. Full pre-payment of the accommodation amount is required in order to secure your booking.  
2.3. Check-in is from 14h00 and check-out is by 10h00. 
2.4. Cancellation Policy per Booking:  
a. Cancellations – for two or less rooms – within 48 hours prior to arrival will be subject to a 
full cancellation fee of the value of the accommodation booked. 
b. Cancellations – for three to nine rooms – within seven days prior to arrival will be subject 
to a full cancellation fee of the value of the accommodation booked. 
c. Cancellations – ten rooms or more – tour cancellation policy in 1 above will apply. 
2.5. Guaranteed reservations i.e., in respect of which full payment has been received or alternative 
guarantees are in place with extended payment facilities at the hotel, will be held for first night only, 
after which the accommodation will be released if not taken up by then. 
 
3. TERMS OF PAYMENT  
3.1. Tour Operators requiring extended payment facilities will be required to complete an application for 
extended payment facilities for each individual hotel. Where extended payment facilities are granted, 
payment limits will be set and must be maintained. Deposits/pre -payments for group bookings will be 
subject to each Hotel’s terms and conditions even if extended payment facilities are granted.  
3.2. A 50% deposit will be requested for all groups of 10 rooms or more, regardless if credit facilities have 
been granted. 
3.3. Extended payment applications must be sent to each individual hotel’s General Manager/Financial 
Controller for approval. 
3.4. All extended payment facilities must be settled in full, 30 days from invoice directly to the hotel 
concerned. 
3.5. Foreign currencies will be accepted at the rate of exchange valid on the day payments are processed and 
paid into the respective hotel’s bank account. 
 
4. WEBSITES  
4.1  Tour Operators who host direct booking channels via their own website must ensure that their o wn 
company logo and trading company names are visibly displayed on all pages. Users of the Tour 
Operator’s website should not be under any impression that they are booking directly with a Legacy 
Hotels & Resorts’ property when using a third party website. Should there be evid ence that any Tour 
Operator is representing itself to the market as Legacy Hotels & Resorts or a Legacy hotel brand, rather 
than its own corporate entity, all negotiated rates will be withdrawn from that company with immediate 
effect.

5. ONWARD DISTRIBUTION 
5.1 The parties agree that the Tour Operator shall not offer or further distribute inventory at the STO rates  
through any consumer facing internet site or other consumer facing distribution channel/system; 
unless the Wholesale rates constitute part of a package where accommodation does not equate to 
more than 60% of the hotel package price. Alternatively a minimum mark up of 20% is recommended 
on the STO rate. 
5.2 Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Tour Operator may offer or further distribute the wholesale rates 
to other businesses   including, without limiting the following: 
5.2.1 Other Tour Operators, Wholesalers or Travel Agents, regardless of the channel used to offer 
or distribute the wholesale rates/inventory; provided further that the internet site, if it be used 
is password protected and the end user/consumer does not have access thereto;  
5.2.2 This prohibition and the contents hereof constitutes a material provision of this agreement 
and any breach/contravention thereof constitutes a breach of the agreement which shall 
entitle Legacy Hotels & Resorts to summarily terminate the agreement and with draw any 
benefits flowing there from. 
 
 
6. DOMICILIUM CITANDI ET EXECUTANDI 
6.1. Legacy Hotels & Resorts (Pty) Limited chooses as its Domicilium Citandi Et Executandi:  
Legacy House, 5 Autumn Street, Rivonia, Gauteng, South Africa. 
Any notices, orders, acceptances, demands or other communication addressed to Legacy Hotels & 
Resorts which: 
6.1.1. Is delivered to a responsible person in the employ of Legacy Hotels & Resorts during normal 
business hours of Legacy Hotels & Resorts at its domicilium for the time being shall be deemed 
to have been received by Legacy Hotels & Resorts at the time of deliv ery; 
6.1.2. Is transmitted by means of a telefax (business days only) to Legacy Hotels & Resorts telefax 
number for the time being, shall be presumed, unless the contrary is proved, to have been 
received by Legacy Hotels & Resorts on the date of dispatch of such telef ax. 
6.1.3. Is sent by email shall be deemed to have been received by Legacy Hotels & Resorts on the first 
business day after receipt. 
6.1.4. The provisions of Clause 5.1 shall not be construed as precluding the utilisation of other methods 
of delivery of notices, orders, acceptances, demands and other communications.  
 
 
 
7. FORCE MAJEURE  
The hotel shall be excused and shall not be deemed in default for any failure of performance hereunder, for 
any reason beyond its control. Such reasons shall be deemed to include, without limitation, war, warlike 
operations, armed aggression, insurrection, riots, fire, explosions, accidents, failure to obtain government 
approvals, Governmental acts or omissions, regulations or orders, natural disasters, acts of a public enemy, 
epidemics, quarantine, restrictions or labour unrest. 
 
 
8. WHOLE AGREEMENT 
This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and no modification, variation, 
alteration or consensual cancellation shall be of any force or effect, unless reduced to writing and signed by 
the parties.

9. GENERAL  
9.1. No suspension of a right to enforce any term of this agreement and no pactum de non petendo shall be 
of any force or effect unless in writing and duly signed by or on behalf of the parties.  
9.2. No indulgence which a party may grant to another party shall constitute a waiver of any of the rights of 
the grantor unless in writing and duly signed by both parties. 
9.3. All costs, charges and expenses of any nature whatsoever which may be incurred by a party in enforcing 
its rights in terms of this agreement, including without limiting the generality of the a  foregoing, legal 
costs on the scale of attorney and own client and collection commission, irrespective of whether any 
action has been instituted, shall be recoverable on demand from the party against which such rights are 
successfully enforced and shall be payable on demand.     
9.4. The provisions of this agreement shall be binding upon the successors-in-title and the permitted assigns 
of the parties 
9.5. All provisions in this agreement are, notwithstanding the manner in which they have been put together 
or linked grammatically, severable from each other. Any provision of this agreement which is or 
becomes unenforceable in any jurisdiction, whether due to voidness, invalidity, illegality, unlawfulness 
or for any other reason whatsoever, shall, in such jurisdiction only and only to the extent that it is so 
unenforceable, be treated as pro non scripto and the remaining provisions of this agreement shall be of  
full force and effect. The parties declare that it is their intention that this agreement would be executed 
without such unenforceable provisions if they were aware of such unenforceability at the time of its 
execution. 
9.6. No remedy conferred by this agreement is intended, unless specifically stated, to be exclusive of any 
other remedy which is otherwise available at law, by statute or otherwise.  Each remedy shall be 
cumulative and in addition to every other remedy given he reunder or now or hereafter existing at law, 
by statute or otherwise.  The election of any one or more remedy by a party shall not constitute a waiver 
by such party of the right to pursue any other remedy available at law. 
9.7. Save as expressly set out elsewhere in this agreement, the agent shall not be entitled to cede or assign 
its rights or obligations under this agreement to any third party without the prior written consent of the 
other parties.  
 
10. COUNTERPARTS 
This agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts and by the different parties on separate 
counterparts, each of which when so executed and delivered shall be an original, but all such counterparts 
together shall constitute one and the same instrument. A counterpart of this agreement in scanned and 
emailed form or which is faxed shall be conclusive evidence of the original signature and shall be as 
effective in law as the counterparts in original form showing the original signatures.
