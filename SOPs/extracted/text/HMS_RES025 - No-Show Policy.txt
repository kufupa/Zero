Document ID Title 
HMS_RES025 NO-SHOW POLICY  
Revision:  3 Prepared By: Systems Development Date Revised: 03/05/2024 
Issue Date:   Approved By: Andrew Wytrykowski Date Approved: 11/05/2017 
 
Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations  
 
Policy: 
 
A no-show is a guaranteed reservation that did not arrive on the date that was booked. 
 
Purpose:   
 
Scope:  
 
Definitions:   
 
Procedure:  
 
1. No-show correspondence 
 
1.1 See Cancellation policy for the procedure – HMS_RES024 – Cancellation Policy.  
 
1.2 A file of the no shows must be kept in reservations with all the correspondence for the reservation.  
 
1.3 At the end of each month a spreadsheet must be drawn up with all the No-shows and Cancellation 
charges done for the month, and filed in the No Show file. The Rooms Division Manager should 
receive a copy of the spreadsheet. 
 
 
Deviations: 
 
Please note that deviations to the above policy & procedure are permitted if the outcome of the above does not 
have a negative impact on the business of the Hotel or Legacy Hotels & Resorts.  All deviations must be authorized 
by the Supervisor or Manager of the Department and must be documented for auditing purposes. The Supervisor 
and/or Manager must use their discretion on the deviation if it impacts negatively the relationship and future 
business from the client, as long as the decision of the deviation is justifiable.
