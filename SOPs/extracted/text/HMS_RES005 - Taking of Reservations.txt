Document ID Title 
HMS_RES005 TAKING OF RESERVATIONS  
Revision:  4 Prepared By: Systems Development Revision Date: 24/11/2024 
Issue Date:  29/09/2014 Approved By: Andrew Wytrykowski Date Approved: 24/08/2015 
 
Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations 
 
Policy: 
 
The reservationist needs to ensure that all reservations are done accurately and actioned in INFOR 
HMS immediately upon receipt of the reservation. 
 
Purpose:   
 
To ensure correct procedure of taking a reservation 
 
  
 
Definitions: 
 
INFOR HMS - Property Management System   
 
Procedure: 
 
1.1 When a guest / client indicates that they want to make a booking, the reservationist is to capture the details 
on INFOR HMS.   
 
1.2 A confirmation letter for each reservation must be sent to all guests, including t ravel agents and tour 
operators within three hours of the enquiry. 
 
1.3 When reservations are made by other Hotel staff, the confirmation letter is to be sent to client and also 
printed and handed to reservations for them to check that it has been correctly entered and completed. 
 
1.4 Below details must be captured for each booking: (The required fields *) 
 
1.1.1. Date of arrival 
1.1.2. Date of departure 
1.1.3. Number of guests: (including children and their ages) 
1.1.4. Source of Business. 
1.1.5. Room Type
