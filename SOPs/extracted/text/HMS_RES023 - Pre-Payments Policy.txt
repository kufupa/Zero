Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations and/or Group Reservationist, Rooms Division Manager and Unit General Manager 
 
Policy: 
 
When you have requested a deposit to confirm the reservation (the full accommodation amount for the 
total stay is required), the reservation department must give the hotel’s banking details into which the 
client can deposit the prepayment.  
 
The reservations department must complete the advance deposit on HMS, allocating the request to 
either rooms or catering, and the date that the amount should be received by.  Daily, the “Deposits” 
report must be printed and actioned should the prepayment amount not be received by the client on 
the day that was specified on the reservation.  The “Deposits” report should be checked on a weekly 
basis by the Rooms Division Manager and the Unit General Manager.  
 
When payment is received and the accounts department confirms the money is in the bank, the 
reservations or front office department must post the amount on the correct reservation in INFOR 
HMS, allocating the deposit to either rooms or catering. 
 
Purpose:   
 
Scope:  
 
Definitions: HMS – Hotel Management System ( INFOR)   
 
Procedure:  
 
1. Payment Procedure  
 
The following procedure must be adhered to with regards to the following payments:  
 
1.1 Bank Deposit: A copy of the bank deposit slip must be faxed / e-mailed through to the hotel; a copy 
must be given to the accounts department.  The accounts department will confirm that the amount is in 
the bank account.  Once the payment reflects on the hotel’s bank statement, the amount can be posted 
on the reservation on HMS.

Advanced Deposit are identified as payments for the reservation posted PRIOR to the arrival date.  
 
These are to be posted:

by the Supervisor or Manager of the Department and must be documented for auditing purposes. The Supervisor 
and/or Manager must use their discretion on the deviation if it negatively impacts  the relationship and future 
business from the client, if the decision of the deviation is justifiable.
