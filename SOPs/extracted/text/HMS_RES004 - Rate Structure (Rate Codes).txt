Responsibilities:   
 
Reservations Manager 
 
Policy: 
 
The Reservations Manager is responsible for ensuring that the correct rate codes are inserted into 
Opera PMS. 
 
Any other rate codes that need to be created must be authorized by the Group Sales Manager and 
created by the Systems Development Department.  The Hotel may not create their own rate codes. 
 
Should a client wish to apply for Organisation or Wholesale rates, this request is to be sent to Head 
Office Sales who will then authorize the request, should the client be eligible.
