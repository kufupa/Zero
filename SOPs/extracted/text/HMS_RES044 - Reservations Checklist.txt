Procedure:  
 
WHEN MAKING A RESERVATION TELEPHONICALLY  
 
Always answer the phone in 3 rings  
 
Ensure that the background is free from noise and any disturbances. 
 
Always be professional and smile when answering the phone – Reservations, good morning/ 
afternoon, my name is __________, how may I assist you?  
 
DID YOU?  
 
• Ascertain dates and check availability accordingly? 
 
• If fully booked, offer alternative dates or place client on waitlist?  
 
• Clarify if client is calling from a company, agent or as a private guest? 
 
• Check if guest has stayed at hotel before? 
 
 
 
• Check whether client is a Legacy Lifestyle member?  
 
 
• If not, explain the benefits of becoming a member?  
 
• Offer to register guest on Legacy Lifestyle? 
 
• Request client’s details, i.e., correct spelling of surname, name,  
telephone number, e-mail address, physical /postal address?  
Legacy Lifestyle is a rewards program.  
It’s free to become a member. 
You can earn and burn points when 
staying/dining at any of the Legacy 
properties or at any of the other Legacy 
lifestyle partners.  
One Legacy Rand = ZAR 1.00  
All we require is your mobile no and date 
of birth to enable us to register you. 
Once you are registered you are entitled to 
a minimum 20% discount off the BAR 
rate, rewards on F&B etc. For more 
information, View website – 
www.legacylifestyle.co.za
