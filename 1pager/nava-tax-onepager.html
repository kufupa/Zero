How it works - built for security and control
  • Hosted in the UK. AWS London Region - client data stays in-country, aligned with UK GDPR and professional body expectations on client data.
  • Outlook-native intake. Watches your shared mailbox, matches threads to clients and matters - clients keep emailing as they always have.
  • Checklists built for UK workflows. Templates for Self Assessment, VAT, AML and year-end - with missing, overdue and complete items in one dashboard.
  • Secure upload links. No client login. Send a link alongside reminders; uploads update the checklist automatically.
  • Human-in-the-loop first. Nava classifies and stages; your qualified team reviews every file before anything touches TaxCalc, IRIS, CCH or Xero.
  • Full audit trail. Every reminder, upload and extracted field logged - scoped access, confidence thresholds and escalation rules throughout.
  • Plugs into the software you already run. Practice management, tax desktop and document stores connected in weeks, not months.
