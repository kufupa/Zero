Nava is an AI tax operations layer that reads inbound client emails and documents, pulls live matter context from your practice systems, and drafts on-brand responses instantly. But it doesn’t stop at the reply - Nava extracts the data and writes it straight back into the systems you already run. In effect, a high-end, always-on practice assistant that absorbs SARS, deal and dispute volume without dropping a single fee-bearing enquiry.

The problem we solve

Today the average response time to a client tax email is roughly four hours - and it’s effectively random. Enquiries land in one queue, so a high-value cross-border structuring or SARS dispute sits beside a routine document chase. Partners and associates open and service each thread one by one, with no way to prioritise the matters that actually drive OWP’s margin. After 6 pm and on weekends, no one is drafting at all.

Why the timing is right

SARS complexity, VAT Modernisation and rising reporting obligations are already reshaping how your clients engage - the ideal, lowest-cost moment to embed Nava. Practice workflows and client files are open and being touched, so adding an AI layer now avoids a costly retrofit later. At the same time, enquiry volume is climbing and peer firms are moving on AI. This is the window to lead rather than catch up - in line with OWP’s own view that technology will disrupt professional services.

How it works - built for security and control
  • Hosted in South Africa. Runs in the AWS Cape Town Region (af-south-1), so client personal information and sensitive tax matters stay in-country - aligned with POPIA and your data-residency obligations.
  • AWS-native stack. Cloud-native architecture on Amazon Web Services, with email transport handled through Amazon SES for secure, scalable inbound and outbound client communication.
  • Human-in-the-loop first. Nava drafts; your qualified practitioners review, edit and approve. Automation is switched on only per-partner and per-matter category, once draft quality is proven - never as a global default.
  • Least-privilege access & full audit. Scoped to only the client files and systems each workflow needs, with edit tracking, audit logs, confidence thresholds and escalation rules throughout.
  • Plugs into the software you already run. Tell us which practice management, document and filing systems you use - we connect in under two weeks, with no code and no vendor API work required.
