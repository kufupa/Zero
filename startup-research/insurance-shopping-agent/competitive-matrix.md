## Gap analysis

- No single “all carriers / guaranteed rate” gap can be claimed: participants have differing panels, states, underwriting and business models.
- Candidate differentiators needing measurement: matched-coverage quote parity, completion through issuance, repeat-entry reduction, transparent consent, specialized risk appetite, or agency ROI.
- None is a confirmed startup capability. Founder product and unique attributes remain unspecified.
