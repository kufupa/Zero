# Customer Review Mining — Insurance Shopping

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## Method and limits

The Exa run returned 20 purposively selected excerpts across comparison services and insurer-direct alternatives. This is not a random sample. Reviews establish that an individual reported an experience—not causality, actual pricing/coverage, prevalence, representativeness, or company misconduct. Platform review counts are not denominators for individual pain themes. Positive examples are included. No usable direct, dated Reddit firsthand source was returned; no broad social prevalence claim is made.

## Repeating themes (hypotheses)

- Repeat forms and redirects.
- Consent/contact control; call attribution disputed.
- Estimate-to-final-price changes.
- Binding/agent handoff may interrupt checkout.
- Coverage comparability is unproven.
- Existing comparison and human-agent services also help some shoppers.

All themes remain **anecdotal**, with frequency/population prevalence unknown. A test must measure journey outcomes and use matched documentation.
