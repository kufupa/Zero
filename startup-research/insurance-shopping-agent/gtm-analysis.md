## Channel opportunity map (directional; no CAC fabricated)

| Channel | Incumbent saturation | Opportunity hypothesis | Cost/risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad “cheap car insurance” SEO | High | Avoid head-on unless unique content/data or partner distribution | High time investment; ranking and brand moats |
| Generic paid search | High | Small capped experiment only after monetization/buyer contract defined | Auction exposure, variable conversion, state-specific compliance |
| AI app directory | Emerging/contested | Could distribute a narrow differentiated workflow | Approval/discovery changes; Insurify present; no proven CAC |
| Embedded trigger (dealer/home closing/lender/benefits) | Medium-high; partner-gated | Strong if founder has access to high-intent audience and consented first-party data | Long cycle, partner leverage, legal/data integration |
| Independent agency pilot | Medium-high existing software | Test one narrow workflow with a reachable agency; charge/pilot based on measurable saved time or incremental bind | Integrations, licenses, E&O/security, agent adoption |
| Local / independent agent referrals | Established | Use licensed partner for complex risks, human fallback | Less scalable, appointment availability, service cost |
| Broad mobile consumer brand | High | Avoid until retention and renewal value demonstrated | Episodic usage, high consumer CAC |

## 90-day validation sequence (research-derived, not user-approved plan)

1. **Weeks 1–2 — access and legal map:** select one model, one state/line, speak to an insurance distribution attorney and 3–5 licensed agencies/partners; obtain nonbinding or pilot terms for carrier access and compensation. No live solicitation before review.
2. **Weeks 2–4 — job test:** replay 10 consented, redacted-by-participant quote journeys across current alternatives; time input, quote availability, final price changes, repeat fields, bind completion. Do not collect/retain unnecessary consumer PII.
3. **Weeks 4–6 — payer test:** offer a concierge workflow/pilot with explicit measured outcome; seek a paid commitment or signed pilot scope, not just praise.
4. **Weeks 6–10 — narrow prototype:** only automate the repeatedly verified step; human handoff and audit trail included. Avoid whole-market coverage claims.
5. **Weeks 10–12 — economic gate:** measure eligible requests, quote completion, accepted referral/bind, cost, licensed service load, refund/clawback and customer value. Stop if unit economics or legal access cannot be established.

## Red Flags

- Paid search and established SEO traffic are not a credible default for a new brand without data on customer acquisition economics.
- AI-platform distribution has platform risk and an incumbent precedent.

## Yellow Flags

- Public competitor channel disclosures are incomplete; channel saturation is qualitative.
- Partner distribution is only viable with written access, lawful consent, and a payable commercial event.
