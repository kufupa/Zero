# Directional Brainstorm — Insurance Shopping

**Phase:** 2 — Brainstorm (exploratory, not selected)
**Project:** insurance-shopping-agent
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Confidence:** Medium on alternatives existing; Low on fit for this founder.

---

The startup-design workflow asks for divergent options before settling on the first solution. These are hypotheses derived from research, not a product brief or recommendation to build all of them.

| Direction | First buyer / payer | Possible revenue | What makes it interesting | Main risks / evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. AI consumer broker/shopper | Auto shopper; insurer/agency funds | Licensed agency commission on bound policies; possible compliant referral model | Large annual shopping behavior; can own consumer experience | Insurify, Jerry, Nsure and others already compare/bind; high acquisition costs; licenses/appointments/carrier contracts; no startup bind economics |
| 2. Quote-to-bind continuity layer | Agency or consumer via licensed broker | Agency software/API fee, or authorized policy placement revenue | Anecdotes and terms show hand-offs, repeated input and final-price changes; outcome could be completed, tracked purchase rather than a list of quotes | Existing incumbents already bind some flows; measure failures in controlled comparisons; legal/product boundaries and integrations |
| 3. Independent-agency AI copilot | Independent P&C agency | Subscription + usage / workflow fee | Agencies already pay for quote intake/rater tools; Canopy publishes adjacent plans at $100/$250 monthly; FurtherAI/other YC firms validate category interest | Existing EZLynx/Canopy/rater stack; must prove saved labor or added binds; likely needs insurance-domain cofounder and distribution |
| 4. Embedded shopping at insurance trigger | Lender/servicer, employer, dealer, finance app | Contracted distribution/API fee or eligible revenue share | Captures user when buying a home/car, renewing escrow, or onboarding benefits; embedded distribution examples exist | Slow enterprise sales, channel dependence, specific referral/commission rules, access/consent/security; partner economics undisclosed |
| 5. Quote infrastructure for AI assistants | AI/platform/fintech or agency | API/usage or licensing fee | Foresee illustrates MCP estimate + browser confirmation; many incumbent APIs are partner-gated rather than self-serve | MCP is protocol, not moat; Insurify now supports ChatGPT real-time quotes in 11 states; data and carrier rights are critical |
| 6. Complex/home risk placement navigator | Homeowners in hard-to-place or climate-stressed areas | Licensed agent commission or agency workflow | Availability and nonrenewal can matter more than lowest price | Home evidence/market scope needs research; Nsure/Jerry/Policygenius and independent agents already support some complex cases; licensing and carrier appetite are harder |

## Current best research recommendation (not founder decision)

Compare **(1) consumer AI broker** against **(3) agency copilot** and **(4) embedded distribution** before locking the thesis. If the founding team lacks carrier/agency access or a consumer acquisition channel, the direct-to-consumer route has no apparent unfair advantage yet. A narrow agency workflow pilot may yield a faster payer test, but this is only an analytical hypothesis and may not match the founder’s ambition or background.

## Red Flags
- “Insurance comparison, but AI” is feature-level copycat positioning; Insurify already brings quotes, coverage Q&A and a purchase path into ChatGPT.
- Six directions are too broad for an MVP; select one buyer, one state, one line and one core outcome.

## Yellow Flags
- Founder-market fit and willingness to spend years on any direction are unknown.
- No product attributes have been founder-confirmed; no Onliness statement should be finalized.
