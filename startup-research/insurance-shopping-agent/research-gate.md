# Research Gate — AI Insurance Shopping (Provisional)

**Phase:** 3.5 Research Gate  
**Project:** insurance-shopping-agent  
**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Confidence:** Medium for market and incumbent economics; Low for founder fit and startup economics.

## Findings

- **Demand:** 53% of U.S. auto customers shopped in prior year; average 3.5 quotes; 48% digital purchases [Data, J.D. Power 2026]. Demand exists, but not proof this product wins.
- **Competition:** high. Insurify, Jerry, Nsure, Zebra, Compare.com, Experian/Gabi, Policygenius, lead marketplaces, agencies and agency raters already cover different parts of discovery-to-bind. Insurify is live inside ChatGPT in 11 states (company-reported); Foresee offers MCP estimates/site confirmation.
- **Monetization:** proven industry archetypes—commissions, paid leads/clicks/calls, embedded distribution, B2B workflow subscriptions. No public commission/CPL/CAC conversion numbers support a startup forecast.
- **Timing:** AI and digital distribution create a tailwind, but the core UI story is already contested. Paid acquisition and regulatory/carrier access are major barriers.

## Verdict: Yellow light / conditional research

Do not pitch a generic AI insurance comparison clone. Proceed only if a founder-specific edge, reachable underserved job and legal/partner access produce a differentiated outcome. Better research candidates: (a) one narrow licensed consumer segment/state; (b) one measured agency workflow; (c) one embedded distribution partner. Which one wins depends on founder access and willingness to spend years on the problem.

## Customer interview gate

Founder requested interviews be skipped/deferred. **0 interviews conducted; no user willingness-to-pay evidence.** Under startup-design rules, later projections stay Stage A, interviews become the first validation experiment, and strategy remains a hypothesis. User can reverse the defer decision.

## To proceed

Confirm first product/buyer and founder advantage. Then run a controlled pilot or customer discovery and quantify one outcome (bind completion, accepted inquiry, agency time saved or partner attach), plus actual permitted compensation.

## Red Flags
- Existing licensed companies have scale, carrier access and AI/embedded distribution.
- Licensing/referral rules and exact carrier access may invalidate the simplest business-model assumption.

## Yellow Flags
- Review pain is anecdotal; no representative or founder customer evidence.
- No unique attribute, unit economics or channel advantage established.
