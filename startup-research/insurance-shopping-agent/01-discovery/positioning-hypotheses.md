# Positioning Research — Early Hypotheses Only

**Phase:** Startup-positioning research (not final positioning)
**Project:** insurance-shopping-agent
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Confidence:** Low until founder confirms product, target user and unique attributes.

---

## Core job hypothesis

A consumer shopping for insurance wants to find a suitable, affordable policy with confidence and complete the purchase without repeating data, losing coverage context, or being surprised by a legitimate underwriting change. Alternatives include direct carrier websites, a comparison marketplace, independent agents, lead/referral sites, embedded insurance portals, AI assistants, and doing nothing/renewing current policy.

## Alternative map

| Alternative | Why a shopper hires it | Limitation / reason to switch (evidence-limited) |
|---|---|---|
| Direct carrier quote | Trusted insurer, direct underwriting/quote, path to purchase | Repeating forms across carriers; no cross-carrier view. Hypothesis to validate, not quantified |
| Insurify / Jerry / Nsure / Zebra / Compare.com | Free multi-carrier comparison, quote and varying purchase/agent options | Carrier/eligibility variation, further underwriting, some handoffs and repeated input reported; each also already solves much of the journey |
| Lead marketplaces (EverQuote/LendingTree) | Broad provider matches, clicks/calls/referrals | Not necessarily side-by-side price or bind; selected complaints describe contact and repeated-entry concerns |
| Independent agent | Licensed human advice, complex placement, carrier knowledge and service | Appointment/availability and slower manual experience; may be best for complex risks rather than a software flow |
| Agency comparative rater (e.g. EZLynx) | One-entry multi-carrier workflow used by a licensed agency | B2B workflow; consumer still interacts with an agent and downstream carrier portal; API/access unclear |
| AI platform + insurer app/Foresee | Conversational research and quote estimates; Foresee adds browser confirmation | MCP isn't coverage/authorization/quote data; final binding/service can remain external |
| Renew/keep existing policy | No effort, continuity and known coverage | May miss alternatives; shopping is episodic, so inertia is powerful |

## Candidate categories to test (not choose yet)

1. **Digital insurance agency / comparison marketplace** — existing category, consumer expects broad carrier access, trustworthy quotes, licensed support, and ability to buy. Highly competitive; Insurify, Jerry and others already occupy.
2. **AI-native insurance broker** — subcategory within digital agency. AI describes mechanism, not unique value; Insurify already markets an AI-native marketplace and ChatGPT quoting. Weak unless paired with narrow segment/access/outcome.
3. **Insurance agency workflow / comparative rating software** — existing B2B category. Buyer expects security, accurate integrations, reliability, human oversight and demonstrated ROI; EZLynx/Canopy and YC-backed workflow firms exist.
4. **Embedded insurance distribution/API** — existing insurtech infrastructure category. Buyer expects authorized quotes, integration, licensing/placement and channel economics; established players sell/partner here.
5. **Autonomous insurance shopping agent** — new-category framing, high education and regulation risk. Do not choose only to avoid competition.

## Dunford 5+1 status

1. **Alternatives:** Initial map above; Medium confidence.
2. **Unique attributes:** **None founder-confirmed.** Potential attributes from research (e.g. MCP, live carrier confirmation, structured comparison) are already available from Foresee or incumbents and are not unique enough.
3. **Value themes:** Possible effort reduction, quote reliability, coverage clarity, and purchase completion. Selected customer excerpts support the vocabulary but do not show paid demand.
4. **Best-fit customer:** Unknown. Auto shoppers, complex homeowners, agencies and embedded distribution partners are all possibilities with different economics.
5. **Category:** Cannot select without actual product/customer.
6. **Trend overlay:** AI adoption in insurance is real but mixed; use it as a distribution/technology tailwind only after proving differentiated outcomes. J.D. Power reports AI use while a substantial minority of users found it unhelpful.

- YC's own startup-idea advice emphasizes working on a problem where the founding team has an unfair advantage and warns about variants of recent successes becoming solutions in search of a problem. [YC: How to Get Startup Ideas](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/8g-how-to-get-startup-ideas)
- For the application, real product proof, customer behavior and founder-specific insight likely matter more than producing a polished deck. YC application advice: [Include a demo](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/J8-yc-application-tips-include-a-demo).
