# Battle Card — Compare.com

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## At a glance
- **Category:** Direct — US digital insurance comparison agency/marketplace; auto-led, also home, renters and pet
- **Product / buyer:** A single browser questionnaire produces side-by-side personalized quotes. Official disclosures say Compare.com has direct real-time integrations with 120+ insurers. Some purchases are completed on Compare.com, while others require an insurer website or representative handoff (official/company claim; accessed 2026-09-30). Official site says agents can answer questions and bind policies. Compare.com is an agency/marketplace rather than an insurer; final premiums can change after fuller underwriting checks (official FAQ; accessed 2026-09-30). Sources: [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/) [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/about-us/faqs) [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/about-us/partnerships)
- **Pricing / payer:** Free for consumers. Compare.com says revenue is primarily commissions and finder’s fees when users buy a policy from a displayed quote, and that it does not sell personal information (company claim; accessed 2026-09-30). Sources: [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/) [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/about-us/faqs) [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/about-us/partnerships)
- **GTM / traction:** SEO/content, direct web comparison, carrier partnerships, and partner integrations/API or custom widgets for businesses. Its partnership page advertises 60+ carriers and API/custom-widget integration (company claim; accessed 2026-09-30). Human support is offered weekdays from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET, and the homepage says agents can bind (official; accessed 2026-09-30). No ChatGPT app/plugin or MCP implementation was verified in this research. Sources: [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/) [Evidence](https://www.compare.com/about-us/faqs) [Evidence… Insurify completed the Compare.com acquisition in March 2023; that announcement cited 7M+ customers and $300M+ consumer savings. These are historical company claims, not current revenue. Current standalone revenue/funding undisclosed in reviewed primary sources. [Company announcement, 2023-03-27](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230327005315/en/Insurify-Completes-Acquisition-of-Compare.com).

## When they win
They win when the user/partner needs auto comparisons, carrier integrations and human support; owned by Insurify.

## How a new startup could compete (conditional; no startup feature assumed)
- Do not claim superiority until matched product/state/risk tests show it.
- Establish who is underserved by this offer, the unmet job, and whether a buyer/payer will commit.
- Compare the relevant measurable outcome: comparable eligible quotes, repeated fields, completed bind, renewal retention, agency time saved or accepted lead quality—depending on business model.
- For this founder, product scope, distribution, unique attributes and customer commitments remain unknown. **No sales objection rebuttal or “why we win” claim is approved.**

## Watch items
- Pricing, funding/current revenue and channel economics may be private or historical; see unknowns in `competitors-report.md`.
- Any public consumer complaint is a single allegation unless sourced with a representative denominator and independent verification.
