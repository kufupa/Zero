# Battle Card — TrustedChoice.com / Independent Insurance Agency Channel

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## At a glance
- **Category:** Substitute — independent-agent referral and human brokerage
- **Product / buyer:** A chatbot asks for insurance type and location, matches consumers with nearby independent agents, and sends the selected agent's contact/profile. The agent handles advice, carrier comparison, quoting, binding and servicing. Trusted Choice reports more than 24,000 independent agencies nationwide. Availability is referral-level rather than guaranteed coverage; terms disclaim agent qualification/licensing, serviceability, contact, appropriateness and availability. Sources: [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/terms-of-use) [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/who-we-are) [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/how-we-make-money/)
- **Pricing / payer:** Consumer-facing agency quotes are generally free/no additional cost. Trusted Choice says it receives no cut, bonus or financial incentive for connecting consumers. The independent agent is paid a carrier commission when coverage is purchased and typically on renewal; commissions can vary by agency/state. TrustedChoice.com is a referral/locator, not the quoting or binding party. Sources: [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/terms-of-use) [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/who-we-are) [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/how-we-make-money/)
- **GTM / traction:** SEO/content pages for auto/home, an agency-locator chatbot and agency profiles; local human follow-up is the conversion and bind path. This is a human/local channel rather than an instant nationwide bind flow. Agent licensing is not verified by the platform. Sources: [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/terms-of-use) [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/who-we-are) [Evidence](https://www.trustedchoice.com/how-we-make-money/) The official site claims more than 24,000 agencies nationwide. No independently verified funding, revenue, quote volume, conversion or bind-volume disclosure was found. ## When they win
They win when the customer already trusts the brand/channel or prefers existing agent/carrier workflows to a new platform.

## How a new startup could compete (conditional; no startup feature assumed)

- Compare the relevant measurable outcome: comparable eligible quotes, repeated fields, completed bind, renewal retention, agency time saved or accepted lead quality—depending on business model.
