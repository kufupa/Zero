- **Product / buyer:** Buyer flow compares offers from 20+ insurers, then assists with switching/canceling the existing policy; the site provides phone/text contact and daily business hours, indicating human-assisted service, but does not document instant self-serve binding. The company describes an AI-based platform used during dealership vehicle sales (Presidio, 2024-08-05). Carrier names/integration architecture are not disclosed on the public pages; claims are comparison across 20+ insurers rather than proof of direct carrier APIs.
