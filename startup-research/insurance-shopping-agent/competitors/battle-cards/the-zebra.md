# Battle Card — The Zebra

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## At a glance
- **Category:** Direct — US independent insurance comparison site/agency; auto-led with home, renters, life, pet and other products
- **Product / buyer:** Browser flow collects ZIP code, driver/vehicle information, and coverage information; algorithmically matches national and regional carriers; shows side-by-side options; and permits online purchase or licensed-agent assistance (official; accessed 2026-09-30). The Zebra says it is not an insurer and earns commission when a customer buys through its agents or from a provided quote (official; accessed 2026-09-30). Some user reports say results are links requiring customers to repeat information on carrier sites (independent complaints; 2025-2026). Sources: [Evidence](https://www.thezebra.com/about/how-the-zebra-works/) [Evidence](https://www.thezebra.com/car-insurance-quotes/) [Evidence](https://www.thezebra.com/about/help/terms/)
- **Pricing / payer:** Free comparison service. Monetizes through carrier commissions and agent commissions when a policy is purchased (official/company claim; accessed 2026-09-30). No consumer subscription price was verified. Sources: [Evidence](https://www.thezebra.com/about/how-the-zebra-works/) [Evidence](https://www.thezebra.com/car-insurance-quotes/) [Evidence](https://www.thezebra.com/about/help/terms/)
- **GTM / traction:** SEO/content-led consumer web, direct quote funnel, carrier referrals, and in-house licensed-agent phone/chat support. Official page says the quote tool takes under five minutes, has 100+ partners, tracks prices, and can notify users if a better price appears (company claim; accessed 2026-09-30). No verified public API, ChatGPT integration, or MCP implementation was found.
