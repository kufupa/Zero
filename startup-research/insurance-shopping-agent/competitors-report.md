# Competitive Intelligence Report — AI Insurance Shopping (Provisional)

*Skill: startup-competitors | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## Executive summary

The consumer insurance-shopping market is occupied by licensed digital agencies, lead marketplaces, embedded distributors and agency/rater stacks. Insurify is the sharpest direct benchmark: it offers free comparison, human help, partner APIs/white-label and in-chat personalized auto quotes in 11 states (company-reported; further rollout planned). Jerry, Nsure, The Zebra, Experian/Gabi and Policygenius claim meaningful parts of quote-to-buy and renewal. Generic “free AI comparison,” “MCP,” and “no handoff” are not differentiated by themselves. Providers monetize free shopping through bound-policy commissions, provider-paid leads/clicks/calls, embedded economics, and B2B subscriptions—distinct models with different operational and regulatory requirements. **Verdict: yellow light for market; no product verdict because founder concept remains unconfirmed.**

## Monetization evidence

- **[Data, SEC FY2025] EverQuote:** $692.5M revenue, including $629.8M auto and $62.7M home/renters. Advertising expense $500.7M; Variable Marketing Dollars (revenue less advertising) $191.9M. FY2025 top two customers represented 38% and 11% of revenue. These establish scale, acquisition intensity and buyer concentration—not a new entrant’s CAC or profit. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1640428/000119312526066957/ever-20251231.htm)
- **[Data, SEC FY2025] LendingTree:** $711.88M Insurance-segment revenue and $1.117B consolidated revenue. Filing describes upfront match/click/call fees for delivered requests and says it exited its insurance agency in Q2 2025. Lead distribution and licensed policy placement are distinct. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1434621/000162828026016084/tree-20251231.htm)
- **[Data, published price page] Canopy Connect:** Scratch/Elevate/Peak are $100/$250/$500 monthly equivalent on annual billing ($1,200/$3,000/$6,000 upfront), or $120/$300/$600 monthly; includes 30/100/300 Quote Points. This is an adjacent agency-workflow price anchor, not a price recommendation for an unbuilt copilot. [Pricing](https://www.usecanopy.com/agencies/agency-pricing)
- Commission percentages, lead prices, bind rates, CAC, API contracts, renewal economics and most private-company revenue remain **unknown**.

## Competitive alternatives by job

1. **Find options and buy:** Insurify, Jerry, Nsure, The Zebra, Compare.com, Experian/Gabi, Policygenius.
2. **Acquire a high-intent inquiry:** EverQuote, LendingTree/QuoteWizard, NerdWallet/Bankrate, MediaAlpha.
3. **Quote through an agency stack:** EZLynx, Vertafore PL Rating, Canopy, agency management/rater products and independent agents.
4. **Existing relationship / do nothing:** carrier site/app, current agent, renewal, employer/dealer/fintech platform, or accepting current premium.

## Potential openings to test—not proven whitespace

- **Quote parity / coverage normalization:** underwriting caveats and individual estimate-to-final differences exist, alongside positive outcomes. Compare identical limits/deductibles with matched carrier feedback.
- **Quote-to-bind continuity:** some handoffs and repeat entry are reported, but incumbents also claim bind/switch/help. Measure full completion and repeated fields by carrier/state.
- **Consent/contact control:** concerns appear in complaints; attribution is disputed. No prevalence or causal claim warranted.
- **AI distribution:** Insurify’s ChatGPT quotes are already live in 11 states; Foresee exposes MCP estimates and authorized carrier-site confirmation. Protocol is not moat.
- **Agency ROI:** existing raters/data tools already serve this workflow; a new copilot must prove incremental labor savings, binds or retention in one agency.
- **Embedded distribution:** established at dealers/finance platforms; commercial terms remain largely private.

## Strategic recommendation

**Do not lead with “free AI insurance comparison.”** Compare three distinct hypotheses:

- **Licensed consumer agency:** requires a reachable underserved segment, licensing/appointments, carrier access, and profitable bind/renewal cohort.
- **Lead/referral marketplace:** has strongest public revenue proof, but depends on compliant consent, buyer demand, traffic quality, and acquisition economics. State rules can restrict purchase-contingent compensation.
- **Agency copilot / embedded workflow:** has identifiable payer and testable ROI; entrenched tools and channels already exist. Pilot a narrow workflow with one agency/partner before building.

Research recommendation—not founder decision: lead with model where team already has privileged distribution or insurance access. If neither exists, test agency or partner access before paid consumer acquisition. YC fit depends on founder-market fit, sharp insight, execution proof and early customer behavior. A market report is not a startup thesis.

## Data gaps and limitations

- Founder concept, first customer, team/domain expertise, licensing, distribution access, prototype and customer evidence unknown.
- No controlled live test of quote accuracy, coverage parity, rate freshness, browser reliability, bind conversion or cost.
- Private commission/CPL/API/take-rate terms remain unavailable.
- Review set is 20 purposively selected excerpts; anecdotes do not estimate prevalence.
- Carrier counts are company claims and not comparable; availability varies by state/product/risk.
- Google Compare closed in 2016; evidence establishes closure and Google’s stated disappointment, not a sourced causal failure story.
- Startup TAM/SAM/SOM and forecast cannot be calculated without customer, model, access and volume assumptions. Premium volume is not startup revenue.

## Red Flags

- Funded, licensed incumbents already occupy direct consumer AI comparison; Foresee also provides agent-accessible estimates.
- Licensing, compensation, carrier access, underwriting and privacy can change what may be built/sold.

## Yellow Flags

- Repeat-entry, price-change and contact concerns come from selected individual reviews; scale and willingness to pay unproven.
- Existing agencies, raters and partners occupy each apparent workflow wedge.
- 13 records span distinct jobs; they are not one apples-to-apples category.
