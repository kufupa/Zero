# Cancellation scope and fees

- Current explicit cancel/release of an existing stay is a cancellation request. A withdrawn unaccepted quote is not. A one-night shortening is an amendment.
- Match property, confirmation, guest and stay to the source. A confirmation is a lookup hint until the native read verifies it. Missing, ambiguous or conflicting evidence needs clarification or staff review; a failed lookup does not establish that no booking exists.
- Preserve the requested individual, room or stay scope. Linked reservations and group headers do not prove permission to cancel other stays. Unsupported or unclear scope needs staff review.
- Show the fresh current status. Already cancelled means no second cancellation. In-house, completed, no-show, conflicting or unknown status needs staff review. `Reserved` is the compatible status for the ordinary whole-stay case.
- Staff-triggered preflight reads the stay and projects available facts; it does not open or confirm cancellation. Scope coverage may remain incomplete. Keep unknown policy and fee explicit: balance, deposit, prepaid status or a zero folio do not establish the cancellation fee. Never infer a waiver.
- A result of `prepared_for_human_review` is a read result, not a completed cancellation or proof that all scope is verified. Display the observed target and status, scope coverage, policy and fee state. Draft only verified facts; do not send.
