# Cancel reply drafts

Before readback: “We received your cancellation request for [guest or stay]. We will confirm the outcome after checking the reservation.”

After fresh HMS readback: “Your reservation for [guest], [dates], shows as cancelled. [Verified cancellation reference, if available.]”

If a charge remains pending: “Your reservation shows as cancelled. We are checking the cancellation charge.” Mention an amount, waiver or refund only when established.
