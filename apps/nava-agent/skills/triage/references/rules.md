# Routing details

- Completed hotel or staff replies, including cancellation confirmations, sent invoices or confirmations, and authorization pending updates: `triage/skip`, no actions even if marked inbound. A bare follow-up to quoted history adds no work. Act only on a distinct new client instruction carried by the selected text; a hotel sender can also relay a new order.
- Read available attachment text when it distinguishes a new agency order from an existing-stay submission or settlement notice. A completed-booking or existing-booking notice with virtual card (VCC) settlement instructions is `triage/skip` for a pure automated notice, `triage/staff_review` when human follow-up is requested or needed. Both have zero actions, including `attach_document` and `voucher_submission`, even if a readable PDF calls itself an accommodation voucher. VCC mentioned incidentally does not disqualify a genuine separate voucher or POP submission.
- Skip OTA/channel notices, template acknowledgements or cancellations, spam, job applications and thanks without a new instruction. Agency format, distribution or a no-reply address alone does not settle intent: a live agency order asking staff to make a new reservation is Create, as is asking to reserve, book or confirm rooms offered in a quote. Side questions do not displace that order. A genuine existing-stay voucher or POP submission, including a forwarded billback voucher or one embedded in an invoice/payment follow-up, is Filing; unresolved stay identity changes disposition, never workflow. Dates, guests or a booking subject alone do not turn a price or availability question into an order.
- **Voucher Create vs Filing (shared decision):** Never route on the word "voucher" alone (Thompsons, Nexus, TWF, Cliqit, other agency vouchers). Decide from current selected text/PDF **document role** (meaning only; no host, arrival-window, or template-phrase regex). VCC settlement notices stay Triage (zero Create/Filing actions). Same-thread confirmation PDF or verified stay hit on Filing: preserve that booking identity.
  1. Current independent create instruction, or signed agency order asking the property to **supply/make** the stay (not an after-booking payment guarantee) → **Create**. Sufficiently sourced order may open editable Create even when lookup is unavailable.
  2. Existing-stay proof/payment (voucher/POP/billback) submission, cited **6–10 digit HMS confirmation**, confirmation-thread reply, or verified same guest + original arrival → **Filing** (bind that stay). Contradictory or multiple hits → `staff_review`, no bound action.
  3. Bare doc with no clear Create role: `hms_search_reservations` once by surname + arrival (the tool ladders automatically; never put agency voucher refs or PNRs in `reservation_id` — non-HMS values are stripped). Single verified hit → Filing (bind). Incomplete/unavailable search: absence has **no weight** (do not invent Create from unconfirmed miss). Explicit voucher/POP submission with unresolved stay → Filing `propose` unbound (null target). Document role unclear → Filing `staff_review`; no ready-to-Prepare duplicate Create. Confirmed zero alone cannot turn POP into a booking order.
- **Staff forwards:** Emails from `@legacyhotels.co.za` / Martha / Kamala / `hotels@` with short notes ("Please quote", "Please assist", "FYI", "FW:") route on the **inner client request**. Resolve agency/company from the inner client sender, not Legacy Hotels.
- A pre-stay notice with a confirmation concerns an existing stay. Preserve any new guest instruction. A selected answer to an outstanding Quote clarification continues that quote with the earlier scope.
- Fresh availability and inventory polls and event or boardroom RFQs are Quote, including manual scopes. Commission, invoice-copy or payment-link inquiries on their own are `triage/staff_review` when staff help is requested, with no actions. Completed replies and informational notices about them still skip. Keep any separately requested accommodation work.
- For an actual existing-stay status or reconfirmation request, use a scoped reservation read/search, not a guest-profile read. If staff needs a lookup draft, use action `type: "triage"` and editable `details.lookup`: `{reservation_id, guest_name, arrival_date, departure_date}`, with the known confirmation and surname, and null for unknowns. Put field specs under the action's actual lookup path. Keep separate stays distinct and report failed reads as unresolved.
