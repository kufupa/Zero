# Amend reply drafts

Clarification: “Could you confirm [missing change or stay detail]?” Use only relevant verified booking details.

Before Save: “We received your request to change [specific fields] for [guest or stay]. We will confirm the revised details once the amendment is completed.”

After fresh readback: “Reservation [reference] has been updated: [verified changes].” Include price, meals or a revised attachment only if established.

If unavailable or uncertain, name the affected change and the verified limitation. Keep completed stays separate from those still being checked.
